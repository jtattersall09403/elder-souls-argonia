# 0026 — Main quest sharpened: visceral stakes, early villain, three endings, Morrowind length, co-design loop

**Date:** 2026-08-26 · **Status:** accepted · **Scope:** `docs/quests/`,
`docs/world/95-build-sequence.md`

Owner review of the main quest following decision 0018. Six directives, all
applied. Content lives in the modules; this records what changed and why.

## 1. Stakes made visceral and legible (owner: "I don't get or feel 'consent architecture'")

The nuanced truth (the Root of Accord's converted consent) stays — but it is
now **the discovery, never the pitch**. The player-facing stakes, landed in the
first two hours with zero assumed lore: *a death cult is murdering the
tree-gods; when a tree dies, the village's unborn die in the egg that night and
the dead lose their way home; nobody can stop them because the throne is
empty.* Delivered by scenes, not codex: the withdrawn Hist and **the dead egg
clutch** in the opening quest (canon: break a Hist's connection and the unborn
die swiftly), the Owing ledger read-out, the empty hall. New binding rule
60-writing §45d: **assume no Elder Scrolls lore beyond having maybe played
Morrowind** — every load-bearing proper noun gets a newcomer-phrased dialogue
topic, Act I is the onboarding, validator-checked.

## 2. The endgame is visible from the first hour (30-main-quest §18d)

Four devices: **Cuts-the-Old-Knot leads the opening attack in person** and
spares the player as already-dead; **recurring sap-dreams** carry her voice and
the sanctuary's imagery from Act I (our Dagoth Ur dream device); the **Hall of
the Scalded Throne** is a visitable place in Helstrom from MQ05; the D5 basin
stays on the horizon as before.

## 3. Endings: three families, named by what the player takes (§24)

Owner directive — the old five (Guarded/Open/Hidden/Unbinding/Pruned) were
institutionally framed and read as dull. Now: **CUT** — enable the severance
and rule the movement as its overlord (believer variant: refuse to rule);
**CLAIM** — defeat the cult and press the claim canon left open (the Eye is a
king's jewel, the Scalded Throne empty with institutions still pointing at it)
— crowned at Helstrom, backed by one of three sympathetic coalitions, each
personified by principals the player has known all game; **MEND** — defeat the
cult, repair the harm, hand the levers to a chosen custodian (or seal the
sanctuary) and walk away, with the Kept Key as its hidden dark variant. The old
families' machinery survives as variants inside these. **Every ending is
earned over a named, Act-I-seeded opponent at the sanctuary** — Cuts-the-Old-Knot
on anti-cult paths, Never-Writes-Twice/Holds-the-Reed on cult paths — with a
genuine speech resolution (skill *plus* evidence, never skill alone) and a
hard fair duel winnable by any developed build. 10-political-frame §8 revised:
the jewel doesn't crown you *by itself*; whether you can make it a crown is
the endgame's question.

## 4. Caius beat applied

Nesh-Deeka's record alterations surface in the MQ20 evidence;
Never-Writes-Twice files the honest report; the handler is purged at the Act
III pivot and **Never-Writes-Twice becomes acting handler**, so the MQ22
loyalty reports land on the one person who cannot be lied to comfortably. The
player can later free, exonerate, spring or abandon Nesh-Deeka.

## 5. Length cut: 32 → 23 core quests (Morrowind main-line band)

Merged: MQ01+MQ02 (processing runs into the attack), MQ17→MQ18 (briefing is
the sermon quest's opening), MQ23+MQ24 (Shadowscale manhunt is the route
quest's second stage), MQ27+MQ28 (planning + crossing), MQ29+MQ30 (Warden is
the Lost City's climax). Moved to the regional side pool with provisions
unchanged: MQ11, MQ12 (leads now four authored, three required; the moved two
raise certainty), MQ19 (feeds ending evidence). IDs kept stable with status
rows so nothing dangles; act minimums, pacing, targets and §21b updated.
Playtime target 25–35 h. The Swampmoth-Affair alternative was considered and
**dropped entirely** (owner).

## 6. No boats chasing boats, anywhere

Owner: rails-following craft is a minefield. The conversion is now **the
manhunt**: the quarry has already sailed; the player reads departures, gossip
and tides, uses the scheduled boat network (our silt-strider equivalent) to
reach the likely landing first, and confronts at a static intercept. Applied to
TA02 (now D-A), RS02 (fixed watcher boats with detection cones, no chasing AI),
MQ10's provision, and the conversion table in 00-overview §4.

## 7. Fan-favourite coverage table (00-overview §4)

Explicit analogues for the beloved premises at Morrowind scripting cost —
Ultimate Heist → TG10; **Whodunit? → new LQ31 "The Bonding Feast"** (closed-
circle deaths at a canon tribal bonding ceremony, one interior, D-A); DB bonus
contracts → SS06; Dagoth Ur's dreams/courtesy → §18d + a final antagonist who
never lies; Helgen → MQ01. Rule: new packets hit this table first.

## 8. Quest–world co-design loop (the phasing gap)

Quests and world-building feed each other ("a bandit camp west of town" must be
*placed*). New **QW loop** (90-production-sequence §65b, mirrored in
20-world-provisions and world plan 95 Phase 11 + Phase 15): per region packet —
world drafts settlements → quest agent drafts local-quest *briefs* (premise,
cast, tiers, provisions; no dialogue) and requests placements → reconcile →
freeze both halves together. Main-quest/faction sites are confirmed from the
published tables in the same pass. Brief output feeds Q4 production directly.
