# 0008 — Retained reference watershed: the Blackrose basin

**Date:** 2026-08-23 · **Status:** accepted (owner may veto before detail
compile starts)

Phase 6 builds one watershed at full detail that ships in the final world
(master plan §85.2). Chosen: **basin 2275 — the southern Blackrose river
system** (59.3 km², extent roughly u 0.08–0.59 / v 0.45–0.89, the province's
largest), scored against every §85.2 criterion:

- **Meaningful river system:** the largest drainage in the province, forking
  into a delta toward Oliis Bay; solved boat lane Blackrose→Lilmoth.
- **Fringe→deep transition:** firm lowland (46%) through jungle (17%) into
  interior swamp and rootland deep marsh (16%), brushing the Naga-Kur deeps.
- **Fixed settlements:** **Blackrose in-basin** (canon lake city where three
  rivers converge — the detail pass must realise that site); Soulrest and
  Lilmoth at its edges via the southern corridor.
- **Historical layers:** Lilmothiit foundations under Blackrose, Imperial
  penal layer with **Blackrose Prison** a day's march south (separate POI),
  Kothringi/Yespest history, Xanmeer country (Murkmire), Murkwood nearby.
- **Current Argonian settlement:** the Murkmire tribal belt — the deepest
  lore density we have (six named tribes plus "scores more").
- **Travel modes:** corridor roads (Soulrest–Blackrose–Lilmoth,
  Helstrom–Blackrose), river/boat lane, marsh swimming, jungle and hill
  climbing; underwater POIs implied by the lake city and drowned history.
- **Asset coverage:** Xanmeer kit, marsh/vegetation kits, Imperial
  fort/prison kits and Argonian hut kits all apply directly.

Runner-up: the Alten Corimont/Shadowfen channel (50.2 km²) — strong pirate/
Ayleid material but thinner tribal lore and only one adjacent city.
