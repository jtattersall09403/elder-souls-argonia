# Regional vegetation variety audit — 2026-09-08

> **Superseded in part, 2026-09-09.** This audit measured SPECIES variety and
> got it right. What it did not measure was between-region *density*. The
> owner's follow-up report ("everything below the mountains looks the same")
> turned out to be about that: the tropical jungle was delivering at
> the 37th percentile of the province's own lowland chunks.
>
> Two things below are now stale:
>
> * the delivered densities quoted here are **per dominant chunk**, which
>   attributes a class's neighbours' trees to the class. The lake's 54.4
>   trees/ha is that artefact; measured per region pixel the lake carries 0.87.
>   Use `worldgen.vegetation_ladder.measure_delivered_by_region`.
> * §2's two views still exclude the aquatics from the "woody + plant" column,
>   which is why a reed monoculture across eleven of fourteen palettes did
>   not show up here. `report_flora_variety.py` now prints a third, understory
>   view that keeps them.
>
> The density ladder, its grounding and its gates are
> [decision 0048](../../decisions/0048-vegetation-density-ladder.md).


Owner, 2026-09-08, after vegetation quietly went province-wide: "it looks like
it may be a little samey between regions … we have a lot of assets available,
so let's use the breadth." This is the measurement, the vault comparison, the
change made and what was deliberately left alone. Re-measure any time with
`python3 -m worldgen.report_flora_variety` (add `--git <rev>` for a committed
version); the rollout itself is recorded in
[decision 0036 § 2026-09-08](../../decisions/0036-phase10-placement-decisions.md).

## 1. How the province got planted

`compile_scatter` compiles every chunk unless given `--chunk`; the terrain
rebuild script `tooling/world-generation/scripts/terrain-chain.sh` (created
2026-09-07 in `98b26b7`) ends with it. The first full chain run, commit
`1b79517` (2026-09-07), therefore added vegetation bundles for 151 chunks:
48 exemplar-ring chunks → 199 chunks, 9.3 MB of bundles. No one decided it;
no budget evidence was gathered; the owner accepted it the next day. The T3
groundcover ring was always province-wide (runtime, from the land-cover
raster), so the only thing that changed was the T1/T2 tree and shrub scatter.

## 2. What was measured

`world/sources/flora/palettes.json` (generated by `worldgen/build_palettes.py`)
is the whole species decision: per region class, layers of one species each
with an authored instances/ha. Two weighted views per region:

- **trees** — canopy, emergent, landmark giant, waterline, basin-mangrove,
  gallery, drowned, fungal-giant roles;
- **woody + plant** — everything except reed/lilypad/kelp belts and rocks.

"Overlap with B" is the share of A's authored weight in species B also uses
(presence-weighted). "Dominant tree" is the species carrying the most tree
weight — the thing the player actually reads as the region's tree.

The breadth is real but narrower than the mesh counts suggest: the 263+349
"jungle tree new" files are ~30 shapes in many colourways, and much of the
rest is temperate (Skyrim/Hoddminir). What was genuinely unused and fits
Black Marsh: the palm families, the second mangrove pair, the stick trees,
the slender "jungle aspen" broadleaves, the umbrella/rain-tree shapes, the
fungi, the dead trees, the water lilies, banana, gorse, alder and pine.

## 5. Rejected on the candidate sheet

Rendered with `pipeline.render_sheet` (frames in
`output/sheets/regional-variety-2026-09-08/`, untracked; re-render from the
kit in a minute):

- `bmv:landscaping/trees/my_own_made/treefern_huge`, `treefern_tall` — the
  NIFs reference `textures/g:/berkians folder/stuff for skyrim/…`; the kit
  builder copies the texture to that literal path, but the colon makes it
  unreachable under Wine/Blender, so the fronds render white. Fixing it means
  rewriting texture paths inside the NIF (a pipeline feature, not a data
  change); until then Tropical Skyrim's `manfern` is the province's tree fern.
  Same applies to the rest of the Berkian `landscaping/` set (cycads, giant
  lilies, pitcher plants) — logged in `docs/phases/P-polish/backlog.md`.
- `bmv:landscape/trees/ethas/paradise-b` (the author's "01Swamptree") and
  `bmv:landscape/trees/scottish-pine33` — both carry crossed flat LOD cards
  inside the L0 mesh, visible at the trunk base.

## 6. Overlaps left in place, on purpose

- **Lagoon ↔ mangrove forest (0.74 / 0.68)** — both are mangrove coasts;
  the meshes are split (`a`+`c` fringe vs `a`+`c`+`d` with a different
  interior) but the ecology is shared.
- **Firm lowland ↔ jungle (~0.85 by woody weight, 0.4 by trees)** — the
  owner's round-7 call that crossing between them must not step out from
  under a 35 m roof; the difference is the lead tree, the bamboo banks and
  the understory, not the roof.
- **Rootland ↔ river/swamp (trees 0.99)** — the rootland *is* the giant
  cypress swamp (0036 Q3); it differs by the fungi, the tree ferns and the
  moss, and by being the only region where `cypress1` dominates.
- The swamp/jungle understory workhorses (`fernlarge03`, `braken`,
  `tropicalplant01`, `bigshrub2`) still appear across regions 5–13 at
  varying weights; their per-region role assignments differ (bank wall vs gap
  thicket vs interior), which the presence-weighted metric does not credit.

## 7. What a later pass could still do

- NIF texture-path rewrite in `pipeline/build_kit.py` to unlock the Berkian
  `landscaping/` set (tree ferns, cycads, giant lilies, pitcher plants) —
  the most Black-Marsh-looking unused plants in the vault.
- Tamira's `New Plants` (Tropical Skyrim) and the `palmtree1-4` family are
  unmeasured; a sheet render would tell in a minute.
- Groundcover (`groundcover.json`) is keyed by land cover, not region, so
  region identity at the floor comes from the land-cover palettes (decision
  0011) rather than from this file.
