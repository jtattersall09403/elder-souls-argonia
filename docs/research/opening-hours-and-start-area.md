# The opening hours and the start area (Alten Corimont)

Reference-game rules for the first 1–2 hours, measured against what we have
actually plotted around the start. Written for whoever authors the opening
packet and for whoever re-plots the Corimont neighbourhood. Not a fence — a
set of ring principles plus a short gap list.

Owner brief (2026-09-03): "a rich vibrant world you want to explore, drawn in
several directions, with the main quest as a signposted option. Not
hand-holding, but don't accidentally ship a terrible first hour."

Related: [quests/30-main-quest.md](../quests/30-main-quest.md) MQ01/MQ05,
[quests/20-world-provisions.md](../quests/20-world-provisions.md) §12 danger
tiers, [world/76 §120](../world/76-stats-progression.md) (vastei must be taught
in the opening hours), [research/morrowind-content-density.md](morrowind-content-density.md),
[research/openworld-place-distribution-and-siting.md](openworld-place-distribution-and-siting.md).

## 1. Where we start

MQ01 *No Name on the Work Roll*: character creation happens during processing
on a **penal work barge** of an *Owing* labour gang near **Alten Corimont**;
the quest runs straight into an Unbound Root raid, a Hist withdrawal, a fire
and a shallow-water escape, and that night the camp's egg clutch dies. Its
provisions name two locations that do not yet exist in the catalogue:
`LOC opening.work_barge` and `LOC opening.work_camp`.

The main-quest exit is MQ05: an **escorted fast-boat convoy Alten Corimont →
Helstrom** (`FAST boat.corimont_helstrom`, a compiled lane). So the opening
ring's job is: survive the raid, get bearings around Corimont, and *choose*
whether to take the convoy.

## 2. What reference games actually do (rules, with numbers)

Distances below are in-game metres. Our province is ×1 scale, ~7.37 km across
and about Skyrim's land area (00-core §8), so Skyrim/Oblivion numbers transfer
directly; Morrowind's map is roughly 1.5× wider, so treat its figures as upper
bounds. Figures marked *(est.)* are inferred from walking time/route
description, not published coordinates.

| Rule | Number | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Safe radius around spawn | ~100–300 m of non-lethal ground | Seyda Neen; Helgen→road; Church of Elleh |
| First optional dungeon | 100–250 m from the hub, deliberately trivial | *Morrowind:Addamasartus* — 3 low-level slavers and a rat, ~100 m from town |
| First safe settlement | 600–800 m, one unambiguous road, no fork | Helgen→Riverwood, one road *(est. ~700 m)* |
| Beats along that road | something every 200–400 m | *Morrowind:Starting Out* / *Morrowind:Seyda Neen*: Tarhiel's fall, the shack, the mudcrab merchant, Fargoth's stash |
| A landmark visible but not yet reachable | 1 far pull, named by an NPC | *Skyrim:Bleak Falls Barrow (place)* from Riverwood; Vilverin across the water at the sewer exit (*Oblivion:Imperial Prison Sewer*); Stormveil from The First Step |
| One vantage point showing 3+ pulls at once | within 50–150 m of spawn | Great Plateau Tower (3 shrines + Hyrule Castle); The First Step |
| Distinct directional pulls | 3–4, no more | Seyda Neen: town, Addamasartus, the Balmora road, open swamp |
| Telegraphed overmatch | on the path, visible from safety before aggro range | Elden Ring's Tree Sentinel patrolling the Church of Elleh road |
| Danger is not scaled down off-path | a real wall that teaches "go where the pointers point" | Gothic 1 scavengers two-shot a fresh character outside the Old Camp |
| Main-quest signpost | a physical, already-visible object, not a marker | the silt strider at Seyda Neen answers "how do I reach the city" without a quest arrow |
| Bounded vs unbounded opening | pick one deliberately | BotW hard-bounds the Plateau; Morrowind/Skyrim/Elden Ring let you walk into death immediately, with dialogue and visible strength as the only warning |
| Tutorial verbs | 3–4 small sites, one verb each | BotW's four Plateau shrines |

UESP pages cited: Morrowind:Addamasartus · Morrowind:Seyda Neen ·
Morrowind:Starting Out · Oblivion:Imperial Prison Sewer (Tutorial) ·
Skyrim:Bleak Falls Barrow (place) · Skyrim:Bleak Falls Barrow (quest) ·
Skyrim:Embershard Mine · Skyrim:The Guardian Stones.

The pattern behind all of them: **the first hour is a compass, not a corridor.**
Every game gives one safe hub, one trivial dungeon almost on top of it, one
optional main-quest object you can see, one far landmark you cannot reach, and
at least one direction that will kill you but says so first.

## 4. Assessment

| Verdict | Item |
|---|---|
| **Missing** | **The start itself has no record.** `opening.work_barge` and `opening.work_camp` are not in the catalogue; the nearest Owing-institution record, The Hiring Yard (`place.pirate-freeholds.corimont-hiring-yard`), is `deferred`. The opening is currently unplotted ground. |
| **Missing** | **No trivial first dungeon at 100–250 m.** The nearest enterable-and-survivable interiors are D3/D4 (Nine Sockets D4 at 332 m, The Underway D3 at 501 m). There is no Addamasartus. |
| **Missing** | **No vantage point near the start** putting 3+ pulls in one eyeline. The nearest ladder/vista towers are Climbs-To-See (548 m, W) and Diggings Ladder (667 m). Neither reads as "the first thing you climb". |
| **Missing** | **No teach-site for *vastei*.** 76 §120 requires a Nisswo to explain it in fiction in the opening hours. The nearest nisswo-rest-house is Northern Rest at 1.35 km. |
| **Wrong** | **D4/D5 at 250–500 m in four directions at once** — The Unmade Work (D4, 251 m), Gate-Side Camp (D4, 320 m), Nine Sockets and The Wading Ground (D4, ~330 m, a marsh-giant ground), The Pulled Field (D4, 393 m), Medusa Wood (**D5 legendary-deep**, 459 m). Every compass direction from the start is lethal within five minutes' walk. That is past "Gothic gradient" into "no direction is a good direction". |
| **Wrong** | **The one road out is band 3 by 250 m.** There is no D1–D2 corridor giving the player a safe first walk anywhere. |
| **Good** | **Ring 1 (100–250 m) is already a proper hamlet ring** — Chasecreek, Bundle Racks, No-Tree, The Trunk Span, the cairn field: four different kinds of "someone lives/died here", all D2, all reachable, all on short boardwalks. This is exactly the Seyda Neen texture. |
| **Good** | **Three genuine far pulls, each different**: Tearmouth (port town, 534 m NW), Stormhold (city, 1.25 km W), Helstrom (capital and main quest, 1.70 km S). A terraced river city on a skyline is our Bleak Falls Barrow. |
| **Good** | **The main-quest signpost is already physical and diegetic**: the MQ05 convoy dock at Corimont. A guarded fast boat sitting at a quay is our silt strider — visible, ignorable, self-explanatory. |
| **Good** | **Beat spacing is fine.** With 14 tracks and ~20 places inside 700 m, no stretch of the local network is empty for 400 m. |
| **Good** | **The Old Pens (D3 slaver-apparatus, 350 m N)** is thematically perfect as the first *story* site for a character who just escaped an Owing gang. |

## 5. Proposed "opening ring" principles

Principles for plotting, not a boundary. They constrain danger and one or two
site types, and nothing else — the player can still walk any direction and die.

- **Ring A, 0–250 m (footing).** Danger band ≤2. Contains the barge/camp, a
  safe-ish landing, at least three inhabited or legible small sites, and the
  first climbable vantage. **No band ≥4.** *We already have the sites; we lack
  the start record and the vantage.*
- **Ring B, 250–600 m (first choices).** Danger band ≤3 in at least **three of
  the four quadrants**, and exactly **one** deliberately hostile quadrant, whose
  hostility must be visible from Ring A (a marsh-giant ground you can see and
  hear before you enter is fine; an unmarked D5 legendary-deep at 459 m is
  not). Contains **one small trivial dungeon** (a 5–15 minute interior, three
  weak enemies, one lock, one reward) and the Nisswo who explains *vastei*.
- **Ring C, 600 m–1.5 km (the pulls).** Unconstrained danger. Must contain at
  least **three distinct destination flavours** already visible or named from
  Ring A: a town (Tearmouth), a city (Stormhold), and the main-quest option
  (the Helstrom convoy). D4/D5 is welcome and should be *telegraphed by
  neighbours' dialogue*, not by geometry.
- **Nothing gets moved for the sake of the opening except danger-band-4-plus
  sites inside 600 m**, and even then, prefer re-tiering or re-siting *one or
  two* rather than clearing the ring. Corimont should stay a rough place.

## 6. Top five adjustments

1. **Plot the start.** Add live catalogue records for `opening.work_barge` and
   `opening.work_camp` on the Corimont water, and un-defer or replace The
   Hiring Yard so the Owing institution the player is processed into exists.
2. **Cool one quadrant.** Move or re-tier **Medusa Wood** (D5, 459 m) and **The
   Unmade Work** (D4, 251 m) outward; keep **The Wading Ground** (D4 marsh-giant
   ground, SE) as the one telegraphed hostile quadrant.
3. **Add a trivial first dungeon at 150–400 m** — the obvious candidate is a
   small flooded Owing store or a smuggler's undercut off the Corimont
   channel, D2, three weak enemies.
4. **Add a vantage inside 250 m** (a platform-ladder tower or the port's own
   roof-walk) from which Tearmouth, Stormhold's terraces and the Helstrom
   convoy dock are all in one view.
5. **Give the Stormhold road a D1–D2 first kilometre**, or accept it and put a
   guarded/patrolled boardwalk alternative alongside it — right now the only
   road out of the start hub is band 3 within 250 m.
