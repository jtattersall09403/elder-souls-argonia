# Black Marsh / Argonia — province dossier

**Canon (UESP Lore:Black Marsh, Lore:Argonian, Lore:Hist, Lore:Shadowfen):**

- Dense swampland of southeastern Tamriel; mer races use the name **Argonia**
  (an obscure ancient battlefield) to avoid "Black Marsh"'s connotations.
- "Lush and threatening land teeming with poisonous plants and violent
  predators"; tropical climate defeats cultivation — "foreign agricultural,
  colonial, and commercial ventures beyond the slave-trade have met with
  abject failure". Natives organise tribally, "only loosely integrated" into
  the Empire. → Vindicates the infrastructure-decay and fixed-danger models.
- Argonians (**Saxhleel**, "People of the Root", language **Jel**) are the
  foremost guerrilla warfare experts in Tamriel; connected to the **Hist**,
  giant spore trees of the innermost swamps, arguably sentient and older than
  all races of man and mer; sap is licked in religious rites.
- Shadowfen (NW region): fetid mire fought over by An-Xileel and Dunmer
  slavers; giant snakes; diseases (swamp rot, droops); source of the
  **Knahaten Flu**; birthplace tie to the **Shadowscales** (Shadow-born
  Argonians raised in the Dark Brotherhood).

**Build implications:** edges settled / interior deadly (danger model);
disease+guerrilla ecology in deep marsh encounters; Hist concentration defines
the heartland culture zone; slavery history shapes the Dunmer-facing north.
