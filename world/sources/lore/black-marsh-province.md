# Black Marsh / Argonia — province dossier

**Canon (UESP Lore:Black Marsh, Lore:Argonian, Lore:Hist, Lore:Shadowfen):**

- Dense swampland of southeastern Tamriel; mer races use the name **Argonia**
  (an obscure ancient battlefield) to avoid "Black Marsh"'s connotations.
- "Lush and threatening land teeming with poisonous plants and violent
  predators"; tropical climate defeats cultivation — "foreign agricultural,
  colonial, and commercial ventures beyond the slave-trade have met with
  abject failure". Natives organise tribally, "only loosely integrated" into
  the Empire. → Vindicates the infrastructure-decay and fixed-danger models.
- Argonians (**Saxhleel**, "People of the Root", language **Jel**) are the
  foremost guerrilla warfare experts in Tamriel; connected to the **Hist**,
  giant spore trees of the innermost swamps, arguably sentient and older than
  all races of man and mer; sap is licked in religious rites.
- Shadowfen (NW region): fetid mire fought over by An-Xileel and Dunmer
  slavers; giant snakes; diseases (swamp rot, droops); source of the
  **Knahaten Flu**; birthplace tie to the **Shadowscales** (Shadow-born
  Argonians raised in the Dark Brotherhood).

**Build implications:** edges settled / interior deadly (danger model);
disease+guerrilla ecology in deep marsh encounters; Hist concentration defines
the heartland culture zone; slavery history shapes the Dunmer-facing north.

## Sweep addendum (UESP re-fetch 2026-08-24)

### The province is not a province — CANON_EXPLICIT

> "The Argonians **do not have a word for their homeland** in their native
> language, in part because **they do not consider it a singular nation**. A
> member of the Gee-Rusleel tribe once used the wide-swamp gesture alongside the
> word **kronka-thatith** (roughly 'everything-egg') to describe the region…
> Many Argonians who live there struggle with the concept of it being a
> 'province', as there is so much varied difference in each part of Black Marsh."
> — Lore:Black Marsh

This is the most important single sentence for our world design and it was
missing from this dossier. **Regional distinctiveness is not a stylistic goal we
are imposing; it is the canonical self-understanding of the people who live
here.** "Argonia" as a unified political object is a foreign idea. Anything we
build that implies a single national culture is wrong at the root.

**The north is not swamp.** Lore:Thornmarsh: "Like the rest of **northern Black
Marsh**, the area has more **temperate grasslands** compared to the swampier areas
in the south." Our biome gradient must run grassland/plain in the north-east to
rainforest and drowned coast in the south.

### The three depopulations — CANON_EXPLICIT

The province has been emptied three times, each documented and dated:

1. **The Great Burn, 1E 2828–31.** An Imperial legate fired a peat bog outside
   Stormhold; the fire ran through an underground network **from Stormhold to
   Soulrest and Gideon**, burned for over three years, vented and exploded
   burning swamp gas, and left Black Marsh "nigh-uninhabitable, even for the
   Argonians". **Entire tribes were lost and countless species driven extinct**
   (the wraxu, the Helstrom Crocodile).
2. **The Knahaten Flu, 2E 560–603.** Began at Stormhold; killed every infected
   non-Argonian; 43 years. Exterminated the **Kothringi**, the **Lilmothiit** and
   the **Cantemiric Velothi** and drove all outsiders but House Dres from the
   province.
3. **Umbriel, 4E 48.** Summoned by Black Marsh's own government through the rogue
   Hist of Lilmoth, explicitly to purge foreigners **and assimilated Argonians**.
   It killed everyone beneath it across Murkmire, destroyed **Lilmoth**, and
   overran **Gideon** and **Stormhold** with undead even though they were off its
   path.

→ These are the causal engine of the province's emptiness, its layered ruins, its
orphaned infrastructure and its distrust — of foreigners *and* of nativists.
Full chronology: [topics/history-timeline.md](topics/history-timeline.md).

### The economy, stated outright — CANON_EXPLICIT

> "Black Marsh simply was, is, and always shall be **unable to sustain a
> large-scale, cash-crop plantation economy**. The Argonians, and anyone else, the
> whole of Tamriel, could live in Black Marsh on **subsistence farming**, just
> raising what they needed." — The Argonian Account, Bk 4

Forty years and millions of gold of Imperial roads, bridges, drainage,
anti-smuggling systems and mercenaries made freight **slower**: three weeks
became two and a half months, arriving rotten. Argonian rafts and rootworms did
it in **two weeks, unspoiled**. Named commodities that *did* leave the province:
**gold and timber** from Soulrest (until the tribes destroyed the mines and
plantations in 3E 427), **saltrice** from Thorn, rice from Hereguard, herbs and
hunting produce through Gideon, and **slaves** — the only foreign venture canon
says ever succeeded.
