# Stormhold

**Canon (UESP Lore:Stormhold via corpus extract):**

- Major city, "largest settlement of the region of **Shadowfen**".
- "Dark history … once the **foothold of slavery for the Dark Elves** in
  Black Marsh."
- Built atop a **Barsaebic Ayleid ruin** named **Silyanorn** ("Library" in
  Ayleidoon).
- ESO-era: capital of Pact-affiliated Black Marsh (2E history, era-tagged).
- Shadowfen context: fetid mire fought over by An-Xileel and Dunmer slavers;
  source of the Knahaten Flu; Shadowscale tradition tied to the region.

## Sweep addendum (UESP re-fetch 2026-08-24)

All **CANON_EXPLICIT** from Lore:Stormhold unless marked.

### Site — the province's only vertical city

- On the banks of **a mighty river that forms the provincial border** between
  Black Marsh and "the scenic grazelands of Morrowind"; the river is practically
  outside the walls.
- The land around is **sunken marshes and crocodile-infested ravines with shallow
  waters**; much of the city stands on the old Ayleid ruins and **wide-open mounds
  surrounded by ravines**.
- **Silyanorn was built on the side of a mountain, around a waterfall that flows
  down into the rest of the settlement.** → Stormhold has real relief, a waterfall
  through its middle, and ravine-severed districts. Our terrain must give it that;
  a flat marsh Stormhold is wrong.
- Architecture is a **mix of Dunmer ornate stone and Argonian mud huts**, with the
  abandoned Ayleid compound repurposed for services — the town **mortuary**, guild
  halls. The Dunmer, during their rule, added **proper stone roads, light posts
  and long wooden bridges**.
- **The local Hist tree stands in the town square, in the middle of a pond,
  ringed by ancient pillars, broken arches and a low stone wall.**
- **Beneath**: a **sunken Ayleid ruin used as an outlaws' refuge**, and an
  expansive tunnel system holding **Stormhold Crystals**. A pre-Duskfall book of
  wisdom about the crystals and "the natural energies of Black Marsh" is **lost
  down there**.
- Neighbours: **Rockguard** (NW), **Tenmar Wall** (E), Morrowind (N, toward
  **Narsis**).
- The **western section** is where the Argonian population lived — historically
  the quarter of the **collaborators** who worked with the Dunmer to raze villages
  and gather people for the plantations.

### History additions

- Before Silyanorn there was **an enormous battle between the Ayleids and the
  Argonians** for control of the region. Legend: **the Ayleids were conquered by
  the Hist.**
- The **War for Silyanorn** involved the First Empire and House Indoril, before
  1E 1033.
- Dunmer rule is described as "**venomous and uncaring**"; surrounding Argonian
  villages warned their own away from the city because **Dres slavers acted so
  quickly on unsuspecting people**. Crime ran amok; greedy Imperial warlords came
  to reap the wealth.
- **The Vicecanon office was invented here** in response — "city leaders that serve
  the same functions as councillors or tribal leaders do in other provinces", with
  magistrate powers (Heita-Meen tried Dres slavers and hung them in cages on the
  Ayleid arches for a year, with exile to follow).
- **Keshu the Black Fin** assaulted a Dres enclave outside Stormhold and raised
  the **Black Fin Legion** here; the **Shellbacks of Stormhold** were the main
  Argonian force of the Second Akaviri Invasion.
- The **Conclave of Baal** is based here, and can locate the ever-moving forest of
  **Murkwood** by reading the Elder Scrolls with an ancient tablet (Lore:Murkwood;
  the tablet lay in the **Vaults of Gemin**, a ruin south-west of the city).
- **Late 3E**: the city prison under mad Warden **Quintus Varus** and his crystal-
  digging "Tunnel Rats" — see [topics/prisons.md](topics/prisons.md). Simulacrum
  ruler **Queen Aphiana**; **rivalry with Helstrom**.
- **4E 48**: "The city was overrun by the undead army of Umbriel despite not being
  beneath Umbriel's shadow. This destruction was considered **light** compared to
  locations that fell within the floating city's path."
- Local idiom in use **across Black Marsh**: "**Host of Stormhold!**"
  Local tradition: the **Fredas Night Stingaree**.

### Notes for authoring

- Stormhold is the **layered city**: pre-Duskfall Argonian, Barsaebic Ayleid
  (Silyanorn), Dunmer colonial, Pact-era civic, and modern Argonian, all visible
  at once and all built into a waterfall-and-ravine site. It is the best possible
  opening region for the player because *the whole province's history is legible
  in one walk*.
- The Hist-in-a-pond-in-the-town-square is a gift: a sacred, public, defensible
  centre that makes the city's politics spatial.
- The mortuary in the Ayleid compound plus the Argonian grave-stake tradition is
  a natural, quiet piece of cultural friction to stage.
