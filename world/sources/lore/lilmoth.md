# Lilmoth

**Canon (UESP Lore:Lilmoth via corpus extract):**

- Major city, "the most centralized settlement in the region of Murkmire";
  colloquially "**the Festering Jewel of Black Marsh**".
- "A merchant city known for the **sunken Imperial villas** built during the
  Empire's hold over the region."
- "Unlike other cities, Lilmoth has no centralized government — ruled by a
  **council of powerful merchants**."
- Murkmire (its coastal region, access to Topal Bay-side waters) is inhabited
  by both Argonian and Naga tribes; Murkmire's tribes joined the Ebonheart
  Pact at its founding (2E — era-tagged history).
- 4E context (Lore:Lilmoth history / Infernal City material, to re-verify at
  authoring time): An-Xileel ascendant post-Oblivion; Umbriel crisis touched
  Lilmoth in 4E 48.

## Sweep addendum (UESP re-fetch 2026-08-24)

Lilmoth is the province's pivot in the Fourth Era. All below is **CANON_EXPLICIT**
from Lore:Lilmoth, Lore:An-Xileel, Lore:Hist, Lore:Umbriel and Lore:Accession War
unless marked.

### Site and fabric

- Built on the **southern point of Murkmire**, in the wetlands of the south-central
  coast and the **river estuary that leads into Oliis Bay**.
- **Walled**: traditional **Imperial walls** for most of the circuit, **Argonian
  walls facing the Oliis Estuary**.
- **Built over shallow water — large ships cannot dock.** They anchor out in the
  Southern Sea and lighter goods in. → A canon reason for a boat-and-lighter
  economy and for the harbour to be a *field of small craft*, not a quay of hulls.
- Despite the Imperial exterior, **ninety percent of Lilmoth's architecture is
  Argonian**: wooden **stilts**, platforms "sometimes extending high into the air",
  connected to one another and ascending from the ground to hover above water and
  marsh. **Imperial houses on the same soil sank.**
- Districts: **Pusbottom**, "the ancient, gangrenous heart of Lilmoth", where the
  criminal underground operates (and where the An-Xileel's political enemies hid);
  **Old Imperial Lilmoth**, mould-encrusted villas festooned with vines, large
  bamboos and sleeping palms, with harmless **shines** (will-o'-wisp cousins)
  drifting among them; intact Imperial buildings near the walls, including the
  headquarters of **Cyrodilic Collections**.
- **Lilmoth has its own Hist Tree despite having no native tribe.**
- A **sunken shrine to Xhon-Mehl the Fisher, the Ancestral Organ Lord of Lilmoth**.
- Outside the north gate: rainforest toward **Bright-Throat Village** and the
  **Keel-Sakka River**; the **Hereguard Plantation** (rice; one of the few Breton
  settlements outside the city); further north, **Blackrose Prison** toward
  Dead-Water Village; the **Xinchei-Konu Monument** in the lowlands to the east;
  the **Ixtaxh Xanmeer** (Ixtaxh-thtithil-meht) to the west.
- Neighbours: **Alten Meerhleel** (NW), **Rockpark** (NE).

### Government

- **No centralised ruler**; a **council of highly-influential merchants** who
  impose tariffs and employ guards to patrol the streets. Murkmire's justice is
  less structured than elsewhere: tree-minders and warchiefs handle their own
  territory, and in Lilmoth **people look to their elders, including retired
  Shadowscales**.
- Earlier the city was ruled by an Argonian **Priest-King/Queen**; Queen **Hellena**
  executed the last one c. 3E 109 and married Prince **Magnus Septim**, who ruled
  as King of Lilmoth before becoming Emperor Magnus I. Warlady **Ioa** (3E 80),
  Warchief **Ulaqth** (Battle of Bodrum), Queen **Artomeda** (Simulacrum) are also
  named. Rivalries: **Archon** and **Blackrose**.

### The rogue Hist and the 4E catastrophe

1. **3E 173** — Lilmoth's Hist "went rogue" and **was purged by the other Hist**.
   A new tree grew from a surviving root fragment; its roots spread through
   **lower Lilmoth**.
2. **Before 4E 48** — the An-Xileel **wrested Lilmoth from the Empire**, defeating
   the Imperial forces and looting the Old Imperial Quarter. They **forbade all
   unlicensed foreigners from entering the city**; unlicensed foreigners were
   **restricted to the docks**. The **Synod had no conclave in Lilmoth, nor any
   presence at all for roughly 400 miles**.
3. **4E 8** — the new tree cut itself off again. It **spoke only to the An-Xileel
   and the Wild Ones**; the An-Xileel took counsel from **no other Hist**, and sang
   to it "in a long, awful cacophonous chant". They excavated the **Ixtaxh
   Xanmeer** room by room, pumping it out and laying magicks to keep the water from
   returning.
4. **4E 47** — Imperial Prime Minister **Hierem** came secretly to negotiate an
   anti-Thalmor alliance and sealed it with a ritual at the city tree involving the
   word "Umbriel".
5. **4E 48** — the tree was **psychotic and had been controlling the An-Xileel**.
   It summoned **Umbriel**. The first victims were Lilmoth's own residents;
   mind-controlled An-Xileel and undead sea-creatures **attacked lukiul Argonians
   and foreign races indiscriminately**. The **"Slaughter at Lilmoth" culminated in
   the city's destruction.**

### What this means for 4E 201 — the single biggest open question

Canon says Lilmoth was **destroyed in 4E 48** and never revisits it. It does not
say the site was abandoned. Our era is **153 years later**. This is escalated to
the owner as
[extrapolation/owner-questions.md](extrapolation/owner-questions.md) **Q1**, and
the recommended reading (a rebuilt, deliberately un-Imperial, politically
haunted city on a mass grave, with the third Hist growing from the second's root)
is set out in
[extrapolation/argonia-4e201-state.md](extrapolation/argonia-4e201-state.md) §4.

### Notes for authoring

- The **stilt-and-platform** fabric with sunken Imperial villas beneath it is the
  strongest single visual in the province and directly serves our underwater and
  climbing systems: a city with a drowned layer under it and a canopy layer over
  it.
- "Unlicensed foreigners restricted to the docks" is a superb, concrete piece of
  political geography to keep or to have recently lapsed.
- Pusbottom is canon's own criminal quarter — use the name, it costs nothing.
