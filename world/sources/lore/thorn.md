# Thorn

**Canon (UESP Lore:Thorn, fetched 2026-08-22):**

- Major city in **Thornmarsh**, northeast Black Marsh, toward Tear (Morrowind)
  to the north. Epithet: "**Jewel of the East** — just as deadly as it is
  beautiful; vagabonds are always eyeing purse strings and the guards always
  turn a blind eye."
- Deep ties to Dunmer **House Dres**: their slave-raid on Thorn earned Dres
  its Great House status; **saltrice fields** in the outskirts profited Dres
  through their occupation.
- Outlying villages owned by the **Archeins** (kleptocratic Argonian tribe —
  same class as in Gideon's politics).
- East coast historically occupied by elven dissidents (Barsaebic Ayleids from
  the west, Cantemiric Velothi from the north).
- Notable locales: Thorn Palace, Conclave of Riana, Conclave of the One, Mages
  Guild, saltrice fields.

**Build implications:** northern frontier city with Dunmer occupation layers,
saltrice plantation parcels (plan's estate grammar), corrupt-guard crime
flavour; our Stormhold–Thorn road is the Morrowind-facing frontier route;
Dres slavery history feeds the north's cultural tension.
