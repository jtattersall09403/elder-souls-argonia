# Thorn

**Canon (UESP Lore:Thorn, fetched 2026-08-22):**

- Major city in **Thornmarsh**, northeast Black Marsh, toward Tear (Morrowind)
  to the north. Epithet: "**Jewel of the East** — just as deadly as it is
  beautiful; vagabonds are always eyeing purse strings and the guards always
  turn a blind eye."
- Deep ties to Dunmer **House Dres**: their slave-raid on Thorn earned Dres
  its Great House status; **saltrice fields** in the outskirts profited Dres
  through their occupation.
- Outlying villages owned by the **Archeins** (kleptocratic Argonian tribe —
  same class as in Gideon's politics).
- East coast historically occupied by elven dissidents (Barsaebic Ayleids from
  the west, Cantemiric Velothi from the north).
- Notable locales: Thorn Palace, Conclave of Riana, Conclave of the One, Mages
  Guild, saltrice fields.

**Build implications:** northern frontier city with Dunmer occupation layers,
saltrice plantation parcels (plan's estate grammar), corrupt-guard crime
flavour; our Stormhold–Thorn road is the Morrowind-facing frontier route;
Dres slavery history feeds the north's cultural tension.

## Sweep addendum (UESP re-fetch 2026-08-24)

**CANON_EXPLICIT additions**

- **Founded by the Barsaebic Ayleids** (Lore:Barsaebic Ayleids lists Thorn with
  Silyanorn, Twyllbek and Archon) — so Thorn, like Stormhold and Gideon, has an
  **Ayleid substratum** beneath the Dunmer and Argonian layers.
- Neighbours: **Branchmont** (SW), **Riverwalk** (W), Morrowind (N, toward
  **Tear**). Riverwalk is also named as east of Alten Corimont, making it the
  natural mid-point of the northern trunk route.
- **Thornmarsh is grassland.** "Like the rest of northern Black Marsh, the area has
  more **temperate grasslands** compared to the swampier areas in the south"
  (Lore:Thornmarsh). Thorn's outskirts should read as **open saltrice country**,
  not swamp — the sharpest regional contrast in the province.
- **Thorn was once razed to the ground.** A **Naga** citizen named **Avumar** made
  contact with a **Dremora** and agreed to its terms; the city was destroyed. She
  used an earthen vessel — her **boiler** — to convert energy from the Deadlands
  into steam hot enough to make contact. → A canon Daedric ruin-layer *under the
  modern city*, and a very unusual method worth preserving.
- **Heita-Meen's story begins here**: a slave in Thorn's saltrice fields, she
  escaped with friends into Thornmarsh, was recaptured by an **Archein patrol**,
  received a vision from the Archein village's Hist, was returned to Thorn, killed
  Councillor **Glathis Dres**, and took the **Archein Guard by duel** before
  marching to Stormhold. Thorn is where the Argonian liberation began.
- 2E 572: when the Second Akaviri Invasion drew Almalexia's levies north, **Thorn
  was left with few armed fighters** — the opening that made the above possible.
- Imperial Simulacrum: **King Tibus**; **rivalry with Archon**.
- Late 3E: some Thornmarsh inhabitants "would learn **basic magic just to help
  them survive** in the rough conditions."
- Regional fashion: the **Four-Spike Wattle** throat-spine arrangement is
  particularly popular on Thornmarsh's coastal shores.
- The **Slave-Coffles of Thorn** silhouette-painting, "described as
  heartbreaking", is a famous artefact of the city's worst day and a placeable
  object with real weight.

**Notes for authoring**

- Thorn's identity is **grassland plantation city on a hard border** — saltrice
  parcels, Archein-owned villages beyond them, Dres stonework, an Ayleid base and
  a Daedric scar, with the road to Tear leaving north. Its crime flavour ("the
  guards always turn a blind eye") is a *consequence* of a city that has changed
  masters four times.
- Because the liberation started in Thorn's fields, Thorn is the correct place for
  **memory politics** about slavery: monuments, grievance, and mixed Dunmer–
  Argonian families whose ancestors were on both sides.
