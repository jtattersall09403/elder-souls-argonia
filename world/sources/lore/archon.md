# Archon

**Canon (UESP Lore:Archon, fetched 2026-08-22):**

- Major city "on the eastern coast on the **Padomaic Ocean**"; historically in
  the realm of the **Lilmothiit** and **Cantemiric Velothi** (elven dissident
  settlers from the north) — explains the Dunmer minority in the demographic
  priors.
- "Surrounded by dense marshland to the north and beaches to the south, and
  near a **river estuary that feeds into the provincial center**. North of the
  city … another river that extended to northern Black Marsh."
- Notable locales: Archon Palace, Conclave of Baal, Conclave of the One,
  Harbor, Mages Guild, resort taverns (balconies over the bay), a
  **Shadowscales Training Facility**. Nut-throwing tree sport; strong-jawed
  beetles, chirp-fish.
- Neighbours: Portdun Mont (NW), Seafalls (NNE).

**Build implications:** our mainland-coast placement matches (marsh north,
beaches south); the estuary "feeding the provincial center" is a target for
Phase 3 refinement (a navigable river from Archon toward Middle Argonia — also
a natural boat route to the interior); Shadowscales facility is a major
faction hook; resort-port character softens its danger locally.

**Confidence:** CANON_FIXED (coast position), LORE_INFERRED (interior links).

## Sweep addendum (UESP re-fetch 2026-08-24)

**Correction to the header.** Lore:Barsaebic Ayleids states the Barsaebics
**founded Archon** (with Silyanorn, Twyllbek and Thorn). The Lilmothiit/Cantemiric
framing above is Lore:Archon's, and both hold: the *area* was Lilmothiit and
Cantemiric Velothi realm, and the *city* was an Ayleid foundation. Archon
therefore has an **Ayleid substratum like Stormhold and Gideon**, which the
existing dossier missed.

**CANON_EXPLICIT additions**

- **Trade.** "Archon is a **major stop on the East Empire Company's south-east
  lines**, with connections to the following cities: **Lilmoth, Soulrest, and
  Tear**." This is the only named commercial network in the province — the
  province's maritime skeleton runs through Archon.
- **Piracy.** Archon's coast "was riddled with pirates" in the late 1E. General
  **Lucinia Falco** sent **letters of marque and temporary commissions to the
  pirates of the Archon coast**; with the **Diamond Marines** they secured the
  south-east and even large parts of Middle Argonia (1E 2833). Archon's pirates
  are a *chartered* tradition, not merely an outlaw one.
- **The Shadowscale facility is closed.** "For an unspecified amount of time the
  Shadowscales had a training facility in Archon, but **by 4E 187 it was shut
  down**. There were plans from the Listener of the Dark Brotherhood in Cheydinhal,
  **Alisanne Dupre**, and fellow member **Rasha**, to revive the facility in
  response to the Brotherhood's decline." → In 4E 201 this is an **abandoned
  facility with an outside faction actively interested in reopening it**. That is
  a far better hook than a functioning school, and it is canon.
- **Glowgill Guar**: a nearby coastal tribe bred them — navy-blue with bright blue
  stripes, reportedly able to **breathe underwater**.
- **Amphibious childhood culture**: local games include **Jaw Beetle Fight**
  (children in beetle costume with jaw-sticks on their heads, wrestling
  **underwater**), **Marsh Tag** (underwater, tagging by sound), **Sink Tag**
  (teams in trees throwing nuts down), **Jump Stick**, **Ironseed**.
- Imperial Simulacrum: ruled by **King Herula**; **rivalries with Lilmoth and
  Thorn**.
- 2E: **Elder Nuetepa of the Three Waters Tribe** was active in Archon.
- Elven substrata run the whole eastern seaboard "between Archon and Thorn, as
  well as Arnesia".

**Notes for authoring**

- Archon should be the city where **being amphibious is unremarkable**: children
  play underwater, the guar breathe underwater, the harbour works in three
  dimensions. It is our best showcase for the swimming/underwater systems in a
  *safe, civic* setting before the player meets hostile water.
- The EEC wharves, bonded stores and counting-houses are Imperial-chartered
  infrastructure that outlived its charter after secession — physically present,
  legally orphaned. Ideal for reuse.
- Two pirate grounds exist in canon and this is the northern one (the other is
  eastern Topal Bay by Soulrest). Keep them distinct in character: **Archon's are
  ex-privateers with paperwork**; Soulrest's are Bramman's heirs in the mangroves.
