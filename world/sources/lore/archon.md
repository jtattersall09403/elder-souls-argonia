# Archon

**Canon (UESP Lore:Archon, fetched 2026-08-22):**

- Major city "on the eastern coast on the **Padomaic Ocean**"; historically in
  the realm of the **Lilmothiit** and **Cantemiric Velothi** (elven dissident
  settlers from the north) — explains the Dunmer minority in the demographic
  priors.
- "Surrounded by dense marshland to the north and beaches to the south, and
  near a **river estuary that feeds into the provincial center**. North of the
  city … another river that extended to northern Black Marsh."
- Notable locales: Archon Palace, Conclave of Baal, Conclave of the One,
  Harbor, Mages Guild, resort taverns (balconies over the bay), a
  **Shadowscales Training Facility**. Nut-throwing tree sport; strong-jawed
  beetles, chirp-fish.
- Neighbours: Portdun Mont (NW), Seafalls (NNE).

**Build implications:** our mainland-coast placement matches (marsh north,
beaches south); the estuary "feeding the provincial center" is a target for
Phase 3 refinement (a navigable river from Archon toward Middle Argonia — also
a natural boat route to the interior); Shadowscales facility is a major
faction hook; resort-port character softens its danger locally.
