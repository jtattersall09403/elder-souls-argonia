# Helstrom

**Canon (UESP Lore:Helstrom, fetched 2026-08-22):**

- One of the eight major cities; located in "the reputedly inhospitable center
  of the province called **Middle Argonia**"; many consider it "the immortal
  capital of the Argonians".
- The interior it sits in is "generally held to be 'impenetrable' by
  non-Argonian armies" (PGE3); after defeat at the Battle of Argonia (1E 2811)
  native armies retreated here and could never be besieged.
- "Notorious for its perilous reputation and the city's guard is reportedly
  dangerous"; its alleys "considered both wonderful and dangerous".
- Local marsh dense, consumed by dark **Heart-Mud**; fauna named for the city:
  Helstrom Ancestor Lizard (speculated Argonian progenitor), extinct Helstrom
  Crocodile (died out in the Great Burn of the Blackwater War; skulls used by
  the Dead-Water Tribe as traps).
- Neighboured by Alten Markmont (SE), Greenspring (SW), Tenmar Wall (N) —
  matches the community map's secondary-settlement candidates.
