# Gideon

**Canon (UESP Lore:Gideon, fetched 2026-08-22):**

- Major city in "the flooded hillscape of **Blackwood**"; "a sleepy frontier
  town that serves as a gateway to the greater and treacherous expanse of the
  province".
- Layered cultures: founded by **Barsaebic Ayleids** who named it
  **Twyllbek**; Kothringi folklore layer; today majority Argonian plus
  **Nibenese Imperials**.
- "From the main city of Leyawiin, Gideon is the first settlement on the
  **Blackwood Road**, which comes in from the west through the hills that form
  the southernmost provincial border. The city is surrounded by steep and
  lightly forested hills to the west and humid marshland" beyond.
- Religious note: the "black city" of the followers of **Seth**. Politics: "a
  kleptocracy of **Archeins**" advises the rulers and governs the outlying
  region. Frequented by hunters and herb dealers.

**Build implications:** our pass placement matches canon (hills west, road to
Cyrodiil/Leyawiin = our Gideon–west crossing); city needs Ayleid foundations
(Barsaebic ruin layer), Imperial district, Archein politics; western terminus
of the Archon–Gideon trunk.

## Sweep addendum (UESP re-fetch 2026-08-24)

Gideon is the best-documented city in the province. Everything below is
**CANON_EXPLICIT** from Lore:Gideon unless marked.

### Layout

- The city's spine is **Blackwood Way**, a large thoroughfare that forks off the
  region's main road at the gate. **Most of the city's buildings are on it
  because it is the only dry ground left through Gideon.** Off it, "the streets
  of Gideon are described as **cramped and labyrinthine**."
- On Blackwood Way: the **Temple of Dibella** (a *Dibellaseum* — a consecrated
  love-nest, converted from Lady Dorcia Martius's manor; once hosted a Sibyl) and
  the **Governor's Mansion**, the seat of power and administrative centre.
- The city is majority **Imperial architecture** but reuses the **Ayleid ruins of
  Twyllbek** — the **city plaza** and especially the **city gardens to the
  south** — and **sections of the ruins are closed to the public**.
- Lowlifes excavated an underground arena beneath the city: the **Ruction Ring**.
- **Warm mud-bathhouses** in town.
- Marshland north-east **floods in from Lake Blackwood** to the north.
- **Castle Giovesse** — ducal seat (first lord Calvus Vanin), prison of Empress
  **Tavia** from 1E 2899 — is **haunted ruins "just in arm's reach from the north
  gate"**.
- Neighbours: **Glenbridge** (NE), **Stonewastes** (SE), Cyrodiil (W).

### Countryside

"Much of the region is **largely uninhabited**, save for the **derelict
plantations and lifeless orchards** overseen by the **Archein serfdoms**", with a
notably large population of **death hoppers**. Kothringi land north of the city
lay vacant after the Flu and became the **Gideon–Leyawiin trade route**; a 2E 582
trade deal reopened the road to Gloommire, and "the roads to Gloommire are often
treacherous and are **closed during monsoon season**" (Lore:Gloomire).
Archein plantations east of Gideon: walled estates with crimson blossoms in the
wall dirt, few fields tended, rotten crops still on wilted vines, leafless
orchards, and serfs "thin, weak, near death" whose children the Archeins sell
(The Argonian Account Bk 3).

### People

- **Gideon's Argonians are majority *Lukiul***: accustomed to Cyrodiilic customs
  and trade, fluent in Cyrodiilic, doing business "subtly with great success",
  and leaning away from Argonian philosophy and tradition. This is stated
  outright by canon and is the strongest identity fact about the city.
- Its Nibenese **share the regional Kothringi devotion to Dibella**; residents
  claim Kothringi spirits linger; fortune hunters dig for Kothringi treasure.
- Gideon's Argonians "know what sort of swamp cuisine [outsiders] can stomach and
  cater to them accordingly" — the one comfortable place in Argonia for a
  foreigner to eat.
- Unruly teenagers fashion their head crests into multiple spikes.
- Distinctive local guar: the golden **Ambergill** (Glowgill) guar.

### The rootworm terminus

**The Underground Express runs to Gideon.** "The rootworm travels south toward
Gideon during the **wintertide migration**… Gideon's stop is marked by the
**abundance of red clay**" — you exit at a spire of bubbles from an underground
stream. This makes Gideon a canon **terminus of the interior root-network**, which
matters enormously for our transport model: the western frontier city is
directly connected to Middle Argonia by a route no Imperial road ever matched.

### Fourth Era

- **4E 48**: "Gideon was overrun by the undead forces of Umbriel … despite the
  fact it was nowhere near its line of trajectory. Gideon's destruction was
  considered **light** compared to other locations on its path."
- **"Later in the Fourth Era, Gideon was an active settlement."** → Direct canon
  that Gideon survives Umbriel and is inhabited in our era. It is the only
  major city for which we have that assurance.

### Notes for authoring

- Gideon is the *only* city where Imperial civic life left something other than a
  prison: a governor's mansion, a courthouse, a temple, a road, an arena, a
  bathhouse. It is the **civic-and-records city** the quest plan wants, and canon
  supports that unusually well.
- The dry-ground constraint (one road, everything on it, labyrinth off it) is a
  ready-made city plan and should be honoured literally in Phase 11.
- Rivalries recorded in the Simulacrum: **Gideon–Soulrest**.
