# An-Xileel

**Canon (UESP Lore:An-Xileel, fetched 2026-08-23):**

- Political party formed during the Oblivion Crisis; primarily/entirely
  Argonian; drove Black Marsh's independence from the Empire, spread
  anti-Imperial propaganda, took prisoners of war.
- Many Argonians credit them as "the sole reason Mehrunes Dagon failed to
  conquer Black Marsh" (they summoned the Hist to repel the invasion per wider
  lore).
- Imperial view: "entirely nativistic… interested only in purging former
  colonial influences," restoring an imagined pre-Duskfall Black Marsh.
- **They coined "Lukiul" ("Assimilated")** for Argonians who lived under
  Imperial ways (Lore:Lukiul redirects to Argonian culture — the term is
  theirs, and it is loaded). Non-Argonian staff are rare, poorly-paid advisors.

**Build implications:** underwrites the quest plan's political frame (party ≠
monolith; strongest in Lilmoth; uncertainty of 4E 201 constitutional position
is canonical space we use). Lukiul tension feeds the League of Open Water line
and mixed-city demographics. The Veiled Reed is our project-original organ of
this movement.

**Confidence:** CANON_FIXED (formation, independence, nativism, Lukiul term);
4E 201 detail LORE_INFERRED.
