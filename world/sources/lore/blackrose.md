# Blackrose

**Canon (UESP Lore:Blackrose, fetched 2026-08-22):**

- Major city "situated **in a lake** in the south-central portion of the
  province, where **three rivers converge** — from the northeast near
  **Murkwood** (the dark forest that ever moves), the south toward Oliis Bay,
  and west from eastern Blackwood".
- "Surrounded in infamy … many have entered its walls but few ever leave."
- Built by the **Lilmothiit** (vulpine beastfolk, extinct via Knahaten Flu);
  Kothringi and Yespest human tribes also lived in the region; now claimed by
  Argonians and Imperials.
- **Blackrose Prison is NOT in the city** — it lies "a day's march south of it
  in northern Murkmire". Built by **Imperial Potentate Versidue-Shaie** (2E) as
  "a distant dungeon to house the most feared criminal and political prisoners
  of the Second Empire", named after the ancient city to its north; ESO-era
  garrison is Imperial (ON:Blackrose Prison). → Blackrose's present culture is
  **Imperial-penal over Argonian/Lilmothiit layers**, not simply Argonian.
- Neighbours: Longmont (SW), Rockspring (NW).

**Build implications:** local authoring must give Blackrose its lake-and-
three-rivers site (there are lakes near our anchor; hydrology refinement
should converge channels there — a Phase 3/6 constraint now on record);
Blackrose Prison becomes a distinct POI south of the city toward Lilmoth's
region; Lilmothiit foundations under the city; "few ever leave" supports its
penal-colony infamy and quest tone.

**Phase 11 site guidance (owner + lore review at the Phase 6 gate,
2026-08-23):** "situated in a lake" + walls + Lilmothiit foundations supports
a **walled island core** (the refined terrain's lake island, enlarged to
~190 m radius for a core keep/temple block) with **most of the city as
stilt/boardwalk districts spreading over the lake** and shore quarters at the
feeder mouths — an amphibious city, not a landlocked one. Design it for the
flood cycle (module 40 binding note): boardwalk decks above wet-season level,
lake commerce by canoe.

## Sweep addendum (UESP re-fetch 2026-08-24)

**CANON_EXPLICIT additions**

- **Red Bramman's bandit kingdom was here.** "Roughly around 1E 1033, the region
  hosted a kingdom of bandits led by the infamous **Red Bramman**, a scourge of
  the Topal Bay and enemy to both the First Empire and Empress Hestra." The
  Imperial Navy reached it by following his **mangrove-screened river from near
  Soulrest** deep into the interior. → The Blackrose lakes are connected to Topal
  Bay by a **navigable, concealed river** that a fleet once sailed. This is the
  most important hydrological constraint on the city after the three-river
  confluence, and it hands us the province's canonical smuggling artery.
- **The Rose was quarried out of Blackrose's own past.** Blackrose Prison was
  built from the **rubble of the ancient Lilmothiit city** by Versidue-Shaie's
  battlemage **Pelladil Direnni**, using an army of **stone atronachs**, **in a
  single day**. Full detail in [topics/prisons.md](topics/prisons.md).
- **2E 560s**: Lilmothiit refugees fleeing the Knahaten Flu in Murkmire **fled
  inland toward Blackrose**; their fate is unclear. → A canon last-stand site for
  an extinct people, somewhere in this basin.
- **Rumours around 2E 582 suggested that Blackrose was in ruins.**
- **The city rebelled against the Third Empire** in the late 3E (Empress
  Morihatha's reign) and **built a large army**. The Empress sent her battlemage
  **Welloc**, who used **vampiric cloud** — a Destruction spell that "displaces the
  rebels' strength and skill to their opponents" — to break it. → Blackrose is the
  only city in the province with a recorded **modern armed rebellion against the
  Empire**, and it was broken by magic rather than by soldiers. Both facts should
  show: a city that remembers raising an army, and a population that distrusts
  battlemages.
- An **Argonian ambassador from Blackrose** attended the Imperial court and was
  mocked by Pelagius III, who "insisted on speaking in grunts and squeaks".
  → Blackrose sent envoys to the Imperial City; it is not a backwater.
- Imperial Simulacrum: **King Claudios**; **rivalry with Lilmoth**.

**Notes for authoring**

- The city's three-layer stack is now fully sourced: **Lilmothiit foundations →
  Imperial rebuild after 1E 2837 → an Argonian/Imperial mixed present**, with a
  bandit-kingdom interlude and a broken rebellion in between. "Many have entered
  its walls but few ever leave" reads best as *a city that swallows people* —
  prison staff, ex-prisoners, deserters, refugees and smugglers who never left.
- Its river network makes it the **hinge of the south**: Murkwood to the NE,
  Oliis Bay to the S, eastern Blackwood to the W, and Bramman's river out to
  Topal Bay. Every southern route passes through or near it.
- The 2E "in ruins" rumour plus the late-3E rebellion gives us licence for a city
  with visible **ruined and rebuilt quarters** at different ages, and abandoned
  siege earthworks in the surrounding marsh.
