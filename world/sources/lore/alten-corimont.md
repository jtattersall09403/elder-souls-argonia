# Alten Corimont

**Canon (UESP Online:Alten Corimont via corpus extract; ESO 2E state):**

- Port town in **eastern Shadowfen** (Leafwater subzone), "east-southeast of
  the Hatching Pools", with a working dock.
- "Mostly run by **Argonian pirates and smugglers** who are neutral in the
  Alliance War."

**Era note (decision 0002):** this is an ESO (2E 58x) snapshot. Its 4E 201
state is ours to author — recommended continuity: still a river-port freehold
of smugglers and neutral traders, weathered but alive (fits the pirate lore of
the province and the community map's 4E 231 depiction).

**Build implications:** matches our placement east of Stormhold in the low
wetland corridor; drives the "pirate-freeholds" culture zone; its access
counts as a minor port (not a safe city) in the danger model; dock implies a
navigable channel — a Phase 3 refinement constraint (river route to its
door); natural smuggling-quest hub between Stormhold, Thorn and the coast.

**Confidence:** GAME_DERIVED (ESO), AGENT_AUTHORED for the 4E 201 state.

## Sweep addendum (UESP re-fetch 2026-08-24, Lore:Alten Corimont)

**CANON_EXPLICIT additions** — the Lore page is much richer than the ESO snapshot.

- "A small **port town** located in eastern Shadowfen … It lies **on the bank of a
  waterway, which provides access to the sea**." Confirms our navigable-channel
  requirement outright.
- **1E 2260** — "Argonian **freebooters** from Alten Corimont were among those who
  heeded the call to arms of **Baron-Admiral Bendu Olo** of Anvil and joined the
  **All Flags Navy**." The ship **Golden Era** sailed with the fleet to Thras and
  never returned; much of that fleet was pulled into **Coldharbour** by the
  collapse of the Coral Tower, and the Golden Era's captain took Molag Bal's
  **Crown of Bones** and rules its skeletal crew for eternity.
  → **This is the town's founding myth and its deepest emotional hook**: a small
  Argonian port that once sent its whole fighting fleet to help all Tamriel, and
  never got it back. That is a far better identity than "pirate den", and it
  reframes the smuggling as what a port does when its navy never came home.
- **2E 582** — "little more than a glorified tavern": docks, a few **Dunmeri-style
  outbuildings**, and one real structure — **a beached ship serving as the social
  hub**. Both the **Mages Guild and Fighters Guild** kept a presence anyway.
  Run by the Argonian pirate **Captain One-Eye**. Deliberately **neutral** in the
  Alliance War *because* of its nefarious trade, which is why the Dominion chose
  it to resupply their invasion — and why One-Eye had the Renrijra Maor leader
  killed and took the Dominion's treasure instead. Pact ships were still allowed
  to use the port despite bitter relations. **One-Eye later sailed for Thras to
  find the ancestors who left with the All Flags Navy** and was lost to the same
  whirlpool.
- **Imperial Simulacrum (late 3E)** — "Alten Corimont had **grown to become a
  small town**." Ruled by **Lord Tibius**; **rivalry with Rockpoint**. Neighbours:
  **Chasecreek** (NE), **Riverwalk** (E), **Rockpoint** (SE), **Tenmar Wall** (W).
  → It is *not* a static hamlet: canon shows it growing from a tavern-and-a-hulk
  in the 2E to a small town under a lord in the 3E.

**Notes for authoring**

- Keep the **beached ship as a building** — it is canon, it is memorable, and it
  is exactly the kind of asset our kit pipeline can produce.
- Its politics are **principled neutrality with a price list**: it will host
  anyone's ships and sell anyone's cargo, and it kills people who try to make it
  pick a side. That is a coherent, playable stance for our era regardless of who
  governs the province.
- The All Flags Navy loss gives the town a **memorial and a grudge against the
  sea**, and a legitimate reason for old Yokudan/Colovian/Breton naval debris and
  heirlooms to be in Argonian hands here.
- Its neighbour list makes it the **eastern anchor of the northern trunk route**
  (Stormhold → Tenmar Wall → Alten Corimont → Riverwalk → Thorn), which matches
  the road network the acceptance rules require.
