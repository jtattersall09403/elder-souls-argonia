# Duskfall

**Canon (UESP Lore:Duskfall, fetched 2026-08-23):**

- "The slow decline of the old Saxhleel civilization" — cause unknown; canon
  keeps it mysterious (wonder budget: keep it so).
- Before it, the **xanmeer period** ("Days of Stone Nests"): ancient Argonian
  society theorised as more advanced and unified; adept metalworking, volcanic
  glass weaponry, possible Dwemer-inspired work; Elder Argonians communed
  directly with the Hist and made bows of Hist bark.
- Ancient Argonian ruins reach as far as southern Morrowind's coast — wide
  trade or wide civilisation, competing theories.

**Build implications:** Xanmeer dungeon family = pre-Duskfall layer; the Root
of Accord (project-original) is framed as a post-crisis accord in this era's
aftermath; pre-Duskfall systems are what the Eye ciphers. Ruin placement can
legitimately be province-wide.

**Confidence:** CANON_FIXED (decline + xanmeer period existence); causes and
dates deliberately open.
