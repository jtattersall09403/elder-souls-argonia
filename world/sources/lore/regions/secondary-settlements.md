# Secondary-settlement gazetteer

Everything below the eight majors. Fetched from UESP 2026-08-24. This is the
placement candidate list for Phase 11 and the "who runs it in 4E 201" list for
[../extrapolation/gap-register.md](../extrapolation/gap-register.md).

**Reading the tiers.** "Lore page" = UESP has a dedicated article. "Arena name
only" = the settlement is named as a neighbour on a city page (from Arena's
1E-through-3E gazetteer) but has **no article**: its history, ruler, size and
character are *ours to author*, which makes them the cheapest legitimate places
to invent. "Book only" = named in an in-universe book, no article.

**Note on Riverwalk**: it is named as east of Alten Corimont *and* west of Thorn.
That makes it the natural mid-point of the **Alten Corimont–Thorn** leg and a
strong candidate for a real waystation settlement on our northern trunk route.
Similarly **Tenmar Wall** is named as a neighbour of Helstrom, Stormhold *and*
Alten Corimont — canon puts it at the hinge of the north, so it should be a real
junction in our road/river network, not a dead ruin off to one side.

## Build implications

- The adjacency table above is a **free constraint set** for Phase 11 placement:
  fifteen named secondary settlements with directions relative to fixed cities.
  Using them costs nothing and buys canon compliance.
- Seven of the nine "with Lore page" Argonian settlements are **religious or
  ruin-centred** (Bogmother, Glenbridge, Hissmir, Hatching Pools, Loriasel,
  Mazzatun, Stonewastes). Village-with-a-xanmeer is the canonical Argonian
  secondary-settlement form; plain farming villages are the exception.
- Three canon settlements are **catastrophe sites frozen in a bad state**
  (Stillrise, Rockpark, Zuuk). Keeping at least one such place is good for the
  "world doesn't soften" rule.
