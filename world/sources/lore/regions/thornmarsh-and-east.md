# Thornmarsh and the eastern seaboard (Thorn · Archon · Arnesia)

Sources: Lore:Thornmarsh, Lore:Thorn, Lore:Archon, Lore:Arnesia,
Lore:Barsaebic Ayleids, Lore:House Dres, Lore:Arnesian War, Lore:Accession War,
Lore:Padomaic Ocean, Lore:East Empire Company (fetched 2026-08-24).

## Thornmarsh — CANON_EXPLICIT

- Coastal region of **north-eastern** Black Marsh. Bordered by Shadowfen (W),
  Morrowind (N), the **Padomaic Ocean** (E). Epithet: **"Jewel of the East"**.
- **"Like the rest of northern Black Marsh, the area has more temperate
  grasslands compared to the swampier areas in the south."** → the single
  clearest canon statement that our north is *not* uniform swamp. Grassland/
  savannah-to-marsh gradient belongs in the region grammar.
- Borders Morrowind around **Tear**, "a coastal plain part of the Dres District".
- Joined the Ebonheart Pact at its founding (2E 572) with Shadowfen and Murkmire.
- Local body fashion: the **Four-Spike Wattle** throat-spine arrangement is
  "particularly popular on the coastal shores of Thornmarsh".
- Late 3E: "some inhabitants of Thornmarsh would learn basic magic just to help
  them survive in the rough conditions." → magic as *survival literacy*, not
  elite training, in this region.

## The eastern elven layer — CANON_EXPLICIT

The east coast between **Archon**, **Arnesia** and **Thorn** was settled by
**Barsaebic Ayleids** (exiles from Cyrodiil after the Narfinsel Schism, 1E 198)
and **Cantemiric Velothi** (Dunmer dissidents from the north). Lore:Barsaebic
Ayleids names Archon and Thorn among the cities the Barsaebics *founded*.
Both peoples are extinct; the Cantemiric Velothi were wiped out by the Knahaten
Flu. → The east has **two dead elven substrata**, and its living Dunmer minority
is a *separate, later* Dres/Tear-facing population, not their descendants.

Barsaebic **Fenlords** — necromancer kings who survived as undead mummies, "said
to have once ruled Black Marsh", still dwelling in the depths of Barsaebic ruins
with the undead slaves they executed and raised — are a canon dungeon-boss family
attested in Shadowfen (Gandranen) and equally usable in the east.

## Arnesia — CANON_EXPLICIT

- "A location or region in the eastern parts of Black Marsh, near Archon and
  Thorn." Little known about the settlement itself.
- A **round island off the east coast** of this area, "which has a dark green/gray
  terrain when viewed from overhead, different from anything seen in the rest of
  the province". → a distinct, canon-flagged island biome for the east coast.
- Lends its name to the **Arnesian War (3E 396)**: an Argonian slave revolt in
  the southern lands of House Dres over the death of the Dunmer trader **Roris**
  (later canonised by the Tribunal Temple), escalating into full war. Morrowind
  won and **expanded its territory south into Arnesia**. By 3E 432 Argonians were
  reclaiming that ground; slavers were still active in Arnesia "by the latemost
  Third Era", even after Helseth's abolition.
- → Arnesia is the province's **contested northern march**: land that changed hands
  twice in living historical memory, with mixed Dunmer/Argonian settlement.

## The Dres relationship — CANON_EXPLICIT

- House Dres's slave-raid on **Thorn** (led by Councillor Thalthil Dres, mid-1E)
  earned Dres its Great House status and made it the primary provider of the
  Dunmer slave trade. Memorialised in the silhouette-painting **"The Slave-Coffles
  of Thorn"**.
- Dres took complete control of Thorn by the mid-2E (Councillor Glathis Dres).
  **Saltrice fields** in Thorn's outskirts profited Dres throughout.
- Beyond the city, **villages owned by the Archeins** (kleptocratic collaborator
  Argonians) — the enforcement layer that made the raids work.
- Slavery abolished in Morrowind by King Helseth (3E 430); the Ebonheart Pact had
  formally ended it in 2E 572, but House Telvanni refused the Pact and kept taking
  Argonians "even from the north".

## Archon — trade and the coast — CANON_EXPLICIT

- "A major stop on the **East Empire Company's south-east lines**, with connections
  to **Lilmoth, Soulrest, and Tear**." → the canon shipping triangle of the
  province, and the only named commercial network in it.
- Archon's coast "was riddled with pirates"; General Lucinia Falco sent **letters
  of marque** to the pirates of the Archon coast in the Blackwater War, and with
  the **Diamond Marines** they took the south-east and much of Middle Argonia.
- A nearby coastal tribe bred the **Glowgill Guar** — navy-blue with bright blue
  stripes, reportedly able to **breathe underwater**. (The Gideon/Murkmire golden
  variant is the "Ambergill".)
- Local sports (all amphibious or arboreal): Jump Stick, Ironseed, **Jaw Beetle
  Fight** (children fight underwater), **Marsh Tag** (underwater, tag by sound),
  **Sink Tag** (throw tree-nuts at the team below). Nut-throwing trees around the
  city. Strong-jawed beetles and chirp-fish.
- Resort taverns with balconies over the bay.

## Build implications

- The north-east is where our world can be **visibly least "swamp"**: temperate
  grassland, coastal plain, beaches south of Archon, dense marsh only north of it.
  That is canon, and it is the strongest lever we have for regional contrast.
- Thorn = plantation landscape (saltrice parcels) + Archein-owned villages + a
  border-security city with corrupt guards. Archon = maritime interface, EEC
  wharves, pirate history, underwater childhood culture.
- Arnesia is the natural home of a **disputed-border quest space**: recaptured
  Argonian ground with abandoned Dunmer estates, an unresolved frontier, and the
  round island offshore as a distinct set-piece.
- Underwater play has a *cultural* anchor here (Archon's children's games,
  Glowgill guar) as well as a physical one — worth using: Archon should feel like
  the city where being amphibious is ordinary.
