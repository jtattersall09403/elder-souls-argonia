# Waters of Argonia — bays, rivers, seas

The province's primary structure (00-core rule 3). Sources: Lore:Oliis Bay,
Lore:Topal Bay, Lore:Southern Sea, Lore:Padomaic Ocean, Lore:Onkobra River,
Lore:Panther River, Lore:Niben River, Lore:Keel-Sakka River, Lore:Middle Argonia,
Lore:Soulrest, Lore:Blackrose, Lore:Stormhold, Lore:Archon
(fetched 2026-08-24).

## The governing statement — CANON_EXPLICIT

> "The province is known for various river systems, **all of which flow into
> Middle Argonia**." — Lore:Middle Argonia

and, from a native mouth: "Everything in Tamriel flows down to Black Marsh."
(The Argonian Account Bk 4). The drainage is **inward first, then out** — the
interior basin is the collector, and the eight cities sit where its water
reaches salt or a border.

## Named waters

| Water | Canon |
|---|---|
| **Topal Bay** (Topal Sea) | Placid bay enclosed by Elsweyr, Black Marsh and Cyrodiil; the Niben's delta empties into it at Leyawiin. **"The bay's coastline to the east with Black Marsh has long been notorious for rampant piracy."** Also fed by the Onkobra, "which supposedly flows from the heart of Black Marsh". Empties into the Southern Sea. Islands: Topal Island, Grand Topal Hideaway, Tideholm, Deepscorn Hollow. |
| **Oliis Bay** (Jel: **Tikmak**; the Imperial name was imposed when the Empire claimed the coastlines) | Large bay of south-eastern Black Marsh forming Murkmire's east coast; **Lilmoth at its mouth** where it meets the Southern Sea. Bright-Throat and Root-Whisper villages on the Murkmire side, on **rocky cliffs overlooking the water**. Rockpark on the east side. Small rivers along its **northern** coast lead deeper toward Middle Argonia. To the **north-west it connects to a river that flows from Blackrose**. North of the bay: Seaspring village and Murkwood. |
| **Southern Sea** ("Sea of Serpents") | Borders most of southern Tamriel; contains Topal and Oliis bays. Lilmoth and Soulrest are the province's only Southern Sea cities (Soulrest sits between the sea and Topal Bay). Pirates here "keep a much lower profile" than in the Abecean to avoid Altmer and Maormer warships; a large share of them are **Khajiit**. Norg-Tzel lies off the coast SE of Lilmoth; **Solstice** lies south of Murkmire. |
| **Padomaic Ocean** | The eastern coast (Archon, Thornmarsh). Most of the Hist's original domain was sunk into it in the Ehlnofey war, "leaving only a small corner left standing, later known as Black Marsh". |
| **Onkobra River** (Onkabra) | **Source near Topal Bay**; flows **eastward** through the Gideon region "along a muddy and dysfunctional road" and into the dark centre of the province. Fast white water near Gideon. A second Onkobra reach exists in Shadowfen, south of Alten Corimont. **Red clay** at its delta → distinctive pottery. The **Onkobra Kwama Mine** was a Dunmer operation seized by rebelling Argonian slaves (it did not end well for them). |
| **Panther River** | Rises in Cyrodiil's Nibenay, crosses the border into easternmost Blackwood, drains into a basin by **Hutan-Tzel**, passes **Rockguard**, and forks by **Greenspring** and **Helstrom**. Its frogs' unique chirp makes them ideal for the **vossa-satl**. Rare fish: Ivory Darter, Onkobra Muskie, and the prized **Pantherfish**. |
| **Keel-Sakka River** | Runs through Murkmire, inland from Lilmoth past Bright-Throat Village. The **Tum-Taleel (Root-House People)** own its bridge and demand tribute from all who cross. |
| **Oortrel River** | Named alongside the Onkobra as one of the rivers flowing into Middle Argonia (Lore:Middle Argonia). No further canon — a free river for us to route. |
| **Red Bramman's river** | "A **narrow, winding river** which emptied into the Bay near Soulrest, its mouth screened by dense thickets of mangroves" — the pirate-lord's escape route, followed by the Imperial Navy in 1E 1033 all the way to his bandit kingdom near Blackrose. This is a **canon navigable interior route from Topal Bay to the south-central lakes**. |
| **The Blackrose confluence** | Blackrose "is situated **in a lake** … where three rivers converge at once: from the **north-east near Murkwood**, the **south toward Oliis Bay**, and **west from eastern Blackwood**." |
| **Stormhold's border river** | "A mighty river that forms the provincial border between the primordial morass of Black Marsh and the scenic grazelands of Morrowind", practically outside the city walls. |
| **Archon's estuary** | "Near a **river estuary that feeds into the provincial centre**"; north of the city, isolated bodies of water and another river extending to northern Black Marsh. |
| **Soulrest's river** | "Just south of the city is the start of a river that drains out into the sea and **travels north-east into the interior**." |
| **Lake Blackwood** | North of Gideon; floods the marshland north-east of the city. |

## What this constrains for our hydrology

1. **Four navigable corridors from the sea to the interior are canon**, and each
   belongs to a different city: Soulrest (Bramman's mangrove river, to Blackrose),
   Lilmoth (Oliis Bay's northern feeders + Keel-Sakka), Archon (the estuary that
   "feeds the provincial centre"), and Gideon (the Onkobra, west-to-east).
   Alten Corimont's waterway to the sea is a fifth, northern one.
2. **Blackrose must be a three-river lake confluence** and one of those rivers must
   reach the sea near Soulrest, because the Imperial Navy sailed it.
3. **Stormhold is riverine and non-oceanic**, on the Morrowind border river.
4. **Tidal reach is long and violent**: the border bridge at Slough Point is
   swamped by "the tide" on a *river*, days at a time. Our flood/tide model
   should push salt and stage a long way inland.
5. Piracy is canonically **eastern Topal Bay** (i.e. the Soulrest / south-west
   coast) and the **Archon coast** — two separate pirate grounds, north-east and
   south-west, not one.
