# Blackwood and Gloommire — the western frontier

Sources: Lore:Gloomire, Lore:Blackwood, Lore:Gideon, Lore:Glenbridge,
Lore:Stonewastes, Lore:Naka-Desh, Lore:Sul-Xan, Lore:Panther River,
Lore:Marsh Giant, The Argonian Account Bks 1–4 (fetched 2026-08-24).

## Geography — CANON_EXPLICIT

- **Gloommire** (also Gloomire) is "a swampy region on the **southern side of
  Blackwood**, in the province of Black Marsh. It borders the **Blackwood Bog** to
  the north and the **Niben Forest** to the west. The city of **Gideon** … can be
  found here."
- Gideon sits in "the **flooded hillscape** of Blackwood": **steep, lightly
  forested hills to the west** (the southernmost provincial border), humid
  marshland to the north-east, **flooded from Lake Blackwood to the north**.
- "Although routes from Shadowfen and Leyawiin exist, **the roads to Gloommire are
  often treacherous and are closed during monsoon season.**" → A canon seasonal
  road-closure rule for the whole western approach. Our flood cycle should shut
  legs of the road network, exactly as module 40 assumes.
- The **Panther River** enters from Cyrodiil through easternmost Blackwood,
  drains into a basin next to **Hutan-Tzel**, passes **Rockguard**, and forks by
  **Greenspring** and **Helstrom**.
- The **Onkobra River** rises near Topal Bay and runs **eastward** past Gideon
  "along a muddy and dysfunctional road", into the dark centre. It is fast — "a
  crashing force of white water and currents that could carry along large rocks
  with ease" — near Gideon, and sluggish in the interior. **Red clay of the
  Onkobra delta makes distinctive pottery**; red clay also marks the rootworm's
  Gideon stop.
- **Slough Point**: an Imperial **census-and-excise** post on the Cyrodiil side of
  the border, with a Commission-built bridge over a black tidal river; **the
  bridge is swamped by the rising tide** and the crossing shuts for days at a
  time. (The Argonian Account; no Lore page — ours to place.)
- The **Imperial Commerce Road** across the frontier was in practice "an endless
  field of reeds that reached up to your chin", solid ground "of a sort", grazed
  and burned back by caravans and regrowing immediately.

## Human and political geography — CANON_EXPLICIT

- The Gideon region was **Kothringi homeland** in large numbers; after the Flu
  their land was left vacant and became **the Gideon–Leyawiin trade route**. A
  2E 582 trade deal reopened the road to Gloommire, causing traffic through
  ancestral Kothringi land.
- Gideon's countryside is "largely uninhabited, save for the **derelict
  plantations and the lifeless orchards** overseen by the **Archein serfdoms**",
  with a "notably large population of **death hoppers**" and the haunted ruins of
  **Castle Giovesse** within arm's reach of the north gate.
- Argonians of Gideon are majority **Lukiul** — accustomed to Cyrodiilic customs
  and trade, fluent in Cyrodiilic, "conducting business subtly with great
  success", but leaning away from Argonian philosophy and tradition.
- **Naka-Desh (Riverbacks)** are the native Blackwood breed: wide faces, large
  eyes, broad webs on forearms and throat; incurious about the outside world
  because they see little benefit in travelling beyond their Hist's roots; fond
  of riddles; were close friends of the Kothringi and loved their music.
- **Sul-Xan**: a Naga cult who worship **Mehrunes Dagon as the Razor Prince and
  "True Egg-Child of Sithis"**, based at the **Xi-Tsei** xanmeer and the conquered
  **Rockgrove** and **Silent Halls**, raiding Blackwood and greatly feared. Jade
  death masks, green facial tattoos, ritual sacrifice of outsiders and Argonians
  alike, soul snares. → The province's canon Daedric-cult antagonist, and the one
  Argonian faction that is straightforwardly evil by Argonian standards too.

## Fauna and hazards — CANON_EXPLICIT

**Death hoppers** (large frogs, notably abundant here), **hackwings** (giant
saw-beaked birds that chop a traveller and return when they've bled out),
**fleshflies** (red, sand-grain sized, will eat you; guards at the border posts
are "well-chewed"), **crows**, **marsh giants** (spriggan-cousin woodland spirits
that fell dead trees and turn the soil; the Hist have a long acrimonious history
with them and tell Argonians to avoid them; their blood is harvested for paints
and inks), **wisps and wispmothers**, **voriplasm**, **swamp leviathans**.

## Build implications

- Blackwood is the **only region where "Imperial" reads as a landscape**: hills,
  a real road, a bridge, a census post, walled estates, orchards — all of it
  half-dead. The visual thesis is *a failed colonial agriculture in a swamp that
  is eating it*, which the Argonian Account states outright: "Black Marsh simply
  was, is, and always shall be unable to sustain a large-scale, cash-crop
  plantation economy."
- The monsoon road-closure and the tidal bridge are canon mechanics for our
  seasonal route model. Use them literally.
- The Sul-Xan give the west a hostile faction that is neither Imperial nor
  An-Xileel, and a reason for the Four Winds of Stonewastes to exist.
- Three distinct Sithis presentations sit within a day of each other here:
  Glenbridge (contemplative Nisswo), Sul-Xan (Dagon heresy), and the Shadowscale
  tradition. That is a strong regional theme for the western approach.
