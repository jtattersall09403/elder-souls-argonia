## Geography and character — CANON_EXPLICIT

- NW region. Borders **Morrowind to the north** and **Cyrodiil to the west**.
- "A fetid mire, often fought over by the Imperial Legion and Dunmer slavers."
- Winding channels, dank foliage, **ruined temples** and **Hist trees** along them.
- Around Stormhold: **sunken marshes and crocodile-infested ravines with shallow
  waters**; a **mighty river forms the provincial border** with Morrowind's
  "scenic grazelands"; Silyanorn was built **on the side of a mountain around a
  waterfall** (Lore:Stormhold). → Shadowfen is our only region with real relief.
- Sub-areas named in canon: **Venomous Fens** (southern Shadowfen, holds Hissmir
  and Loriasel), **Leafwater** (eastern, holds Alten Corimont), **Deep Marshes**.
- The **Great Burn** (1E 2828) started here: a peat bog outside Stormhold fired by
  Imperial sappers, connected to "a vast underground network that stretches
  across the western portion of Black Marsh, from Stormhold to Soulrest and
  Gideon" — burned three years, drove species extinct, made the province nearly
  uninhabitable even for Argonians. → **Burn-scar geology and peat is a canon
  feature of the western half**, not invention.
- Travel infrastructure attested: a **stone-flagged road/causeway** from Stormhold
  toward Bogmother, "on ground firmer than a Breton thoroughfare" (Improved
  Emperor's Guide, 2E 581) — an ancient, non-Imperial causeway network.

## Danger and disease — CANON_EXPLICIT

- Local creatures: **giant snakes** ("Ample Serpents", kaj-thux), **fleshflies**,
  **wamasu**, crocodiles two men long attacking inside town limits.
- Endemic diseases: **blood rot, the droops, swamp fever** (also swamp rot, rust
  chancre, greenspore province-wide).
- **Source of the Knahaten Flu** (Stormhold, 2E 560–603): killed all infected
  non-Argonians; ~43 years; wiped out the Kothringi.
- **Stormhold Crystals** — rare volatile blue crystals in the tunnels beneath
  Stormhold, "created by the priests of a nearly forgotten religion", possibly
  Barsaebic Ayleid in origin. Filled crystals hold enchantments (healing, buffs,
  teleportation, instant kills); Hollow crystals *store corruption/evil*. The
  **Storm Crystal** is the great one. A pre-Duskfall book of wisdom about the
  crystals and "the natural energies of Black Marsh" is **lost in the labyrinthine
  caverns beneath Stormhold**. → A ready-made dungeon-and-macguffin family.

## Culture — CANON_EXPLICIT

- Argonians of Shadowfen are traditionally **born beneath the great Hist tree at
  the Hatching Pools**, tended by the **Keepers of the Shell** (who keep meticulous
  egg records including star signs). This centralises hatching for the region.
- Home of the **Shadowscales** (born under the Shadow); Shadowscale enclave in the
  deep marsh attested (Improved Emperor's Guide).
- Shadowfen Argonians build **mud huts** (wattle-and-daub with a log skeleton);
  Murkmire builds in reeds. Feathers, bright colours and lizard hides decorate.
- Stormhold tradition: the **Fredas Night Stingaree** (lacquered wasp thoraxes worn
  on tails; the jig "Dancing on Eggs" on a silver slide flute).
- **Hissmir** New Life tradition: the **Fish Boon Feast** (those who can fish feed
  those who cannot) and **Hissmir Fish-Eye Rye**.

## Build implications

- Shadowfen is the region where **stone layers are thickest**: Ayleid (Silyanorn,
  Loriasel, Gandranen), Daedric (Ten-Maur-Wolk), pre-Duskfall xanmeer (Bogmother,
  Hissmir, Mazzatun, Hatching Pools) *and* Dunmer colonial. Our northern culture
  zone should read as **layered occupation**, not "Argonian village + ruins".
- Relief exists here and only here: waterfall, mountain flank, ravines. The
  hydrology/terrain pass should preserve that (Stormhold is not flat marsh).
- The Hatching Pools give the region a single, defensible, sacred, *strategic*
  site — the natural target of any hostile actor, as ESO already proved.
- Peat + underground fire network is a real hazard system for encounters/hazards
  (gas vents, burn scars, subsidence) and explains western deforestation.
- Hissmir is the canon answer to "where does a Hist-less outsider or lukiul go to
  touch the Hist?" — a first-class quest/pilgrimage location.
