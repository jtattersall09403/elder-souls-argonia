# Middle Argonia — the interior

Sources: Lore:Middle Argonia, Lore:Helstrom, Lore:Hist, Lore:Argonian,
Lore:Murkwood, Lore:Panther River, Lore:Rootworm, The Argonian Account Bk 3–4,
Lore:Blackwater War (fetched 2026-08-24).

## The interior as canon describes it — CANON_EXPLICIT

- The centre of the province: "the impenetrable centre, and the dark heart of
  Black Marsh, where only the Argonians can survive." Holds **Helstrom**.
- The marsh is very dense and contains **Helstrom Heart-Mud**, pitch black.
- **"The province is known for various river systems, all of which flow into
  Middle Argonia"** — named examples the **Oortrel** and **Onkobra**, near Gideon
  and Shadowfen. → The interior is a *sink*, not a divide. Our hydrology should
  drain inward toward a central basin before finding the sea, which also matches
  "Everything in Tamriel flows down to Black Marsh."
- **The Hist grow in the innermost region**, and because of that several tribes
  exist *exclusively* in Middle Argonia.
- Militarily: siege weapons could not traverse the dense marsh; the Empire
  "refused to follow nor ever intended to siege" Helstrom after 1E 2811, and its
  advance from Gideon ground to a halt outside the core in 1E 2834.
- "Argonian **feather-serpents** are said to inhabit the depths of Black Marsh."

## The interior breeds — CANON_EXPLICIT

Middle Argonia's exclusive Argonian morphologies (Lore:Middle Argonia; The
Argonian Account Bk 4 gives them character):

| Breed | Canon description |
|---|---|
| **Agaceph** | Needle-faced, "a whole rainbow of scales" — ruby, lemon, bright green, gem-like purples, blues, oranges. Travel by **razor-thin one-person rafts poled with the tail**. Educated (looted a dead Maran monastery's library at Umpholo); accomplished **Illusionists**, like "most simple day labourers in Black Marsh". Sardonic, incurious about the outside: "You're in the middle of everywhere, and near nowhere." |
| **Paatru** | Toad-like. Would have killed an Imperial on the spot. |
| **Sarpa** | Winged. Likewise hostile. |
| **Naga** | Adder-like, 7–8 ft, needle fangs; also found in Murkmire. Historically road-robbers on Imperial plantation routes; "following the cessation of these efforts, they have since left the roads and gone back into the inner swamps." **The majority of the province's native brigands and highwaymen are tribeless Naga.** |

Other physiologically distinct breeds named on Lore:Argonian: **Hapsleet**,
**Mihuitleel**, **Naka-Desh** (Riverbacks, Blackwood — wide faces, large eyes,
broad webs on forearms and throat; use osheeja gars to catch fish; care only for
riddles as cultural exchange).

## Travel in the interior — CANON_EXPLICIT

- **The Underground Express**: **rootworms** — translucent eight-foot worms that
  swallow travellers whole and carry them at speed through **tunnels formed by
  the roots of Hist trees**. Slow digestion ("you could live in a rootworm's belly
  for months"). There is a **wintertide migration due south**; you know you are
  near Gideon by **red clay**, and you exit at **a spire of bubbles** from an
  underground stream. Argonians ride them routinely.
- **Personal rafts.** After the Empire's road programme collapsed, Argonians
  "began once again to use the old ways, their personal rafts and sometimes the
  Underground Express, to transport the grain in small quantities. It took a
  third of the time, two weeks, to arrive in Cyrodiil, none of it rotten."
- **Villages move.** "Most villages in Argonia don't stay put for very long, and
  there's a good chance Hixinoag will drift right down to the gates of Gideon.
  Might take you a month or two." → Floating/drifting settlements are canon, not
  a flourish. Alten Meerhleel is also called a floating village.
- **Lizard-steeds**: the Hist shaped lizards into the forms of horses, wolves,
  senche, bears, guar and camels because normal mounts get stuck in mud, catch
  sloughskin from fleshflies, or eat poisonous flora and die. Bright-Throats breed
  their own horse-lizards; a deep-swamp frost-tribe breeds **Chilling
  Senche-Lizards** from a Hist "known to drip with icicles".

## Murkwood — CANON_EXPLICIT

"The legendary dark forest that ever moves", **on a river, north-east of
Blackrose and west of Seaspring**. Its current position can only be found by
reading the Elder Scrolls with an ancient tablet — done by the **Conclave of
Baal**, an organisation based in **Stormhold**. Home to many **medusas**.
Argonian farmers trade sacks of **fellrunner feathers** with hunters; a single
armband of fellrunner down lets a hunter move through Murkwood undetected.

## Notable interior places — CANON_EXPLICIT

- **Helstrom** and its named neighbours: Alten Markmont (SE), Greenspring (SW),
  Tenmar Wall (N) — the last is Ten-Maur-Wolk, a Daedric ruin, so "north of
  Helstrom" and "in Shadowfen" both hold; the interior and Shadowfen meet there.
- **Hutan-Tzel / Rockguard**, NW of Helstrom on the **Panther River** where it
  crosses from Cyrodiil; an active Argonian settlement with its own tribe, elder
  naheesh and totem-runes; the nearby xanmeer **Xal Irasotl**.
- **Umpholo / Umphollo** — site of a Temple of Mara built ~3E 378; the missionaries
  all died of swamp rot in the first month, "but they left behind some excellent
  books"; by 3E 399 the last book had been stolen from the deserted monastery.
- **Hixinoag** — a small half-submerged drifting village on the northern caravan
  route (The Argonian Account; no Lore page).
- **The great forest of full-grown Hist trees**, seen by Scotti in the deep
  interior — a canon *forest*, not scattered individuals.
- **Vast complicated ruins of the Kothringi capitals** in the interior — a
  significant, largely unused canon ruin family. The Kothringi are extinct; their
  capitals are not in the coastal strip but *inland*.

## Build implications

- Interior travel is **rootworm + raft + drifting village**, not roads. This is
  the strongest justification we have for the "waterways are the roads" rule and
  for a rootworm-based fast-travel/quest device (already used by the quest plan
  to reach Helstrom early — canon supports it exactly).
- The interior is not empty: it holds exclusive tribes, a Hist forest, Kothringi
  capital ruins, Murkwood, and hostile Naga. Its danger is *fixed and social*
  (Paatru and Sarpa kill on sight; Agacephs help), not just environmental.
- Middle Argonia as a drainage sink gives us a physical reason the deep basin is
  both the wettest and the most nutrient-rich place in the province — and why the
  Hist grow there.
- Murkwood being *mobile* is a gift and a hazard: it can be a location that is
  not at fixed coordinates. Recommend treating it as a hand-authored site whose
  entrance is discovered via the Conclave of Baal at Stormhold (canon route).
