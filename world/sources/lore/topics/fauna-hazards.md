# Fauna, flora and hazards of Argonia

Feeds Phase 13 (ecology/encounters/loot) and the danger model. Sources:
Lore:Black Marsh, Lore:Shadowfen, Lore:Murkmire, Lore:Middle Argonia,
Lore:Wamasu, Lore:Miregaunt, Lore:Marsh Giant, Lore:Voriplasm,
Lore:Swamp Leviathan, Lore:Haj Mota, Lore:Rootworm, Lore:Lizard-Steed,
Lore:Oliis Bay, Lore:Argonian, Lore:Jasper, The Improved Emperor's Guide to
Tamriel/Black Marsh, The Argonian Account (fetched 2026-08-24).

## Apex and set-piece creatures — CANON_EXPLICIT

| Creature | Canon |
|---|---|
| **Wamasu** ("swamp dragon") | Large reptilian, **lightning-powered**, tough hide, luminescent organs. Bones are black and hold a charge for years. **Eggs emit lightning while the embryo grows and are ready to hatch when the lightning stops.** Adult males cannibalise juveniles. A Nord account: a champion "fell, flashes and sparks of energy consuming him and our party and cursing all the water to deadly convulsions" — it **electrifies the water around it**. Hide and organs are valuable across Tamriel (tarps, lanterns); handlers use **Hist-sap-insulated prods**. The legendary one is **Haynekhtnamet** of Shadowfen, to whom locals made offerings hoping to stop the seasonal flooding. |
| **Swamp Leviathan** | "A mountain of spines, lying on nine-foot-long claws", white blind eyes, tusks clotted with gore. "Very, very dangerous." Their skeletons litter Deepmire. |
| **Miregaunt** | Large tree-like creatures, mostly Murkmire; folklore says they are as old as Argonians and related to the Hist — either summoned by a Hist to guard, defend or avenge, or an accidental by-product of Hist activity with no purpose. Built of plant matter plus mud, stone, vines and **pieces of ancient architecture**. Each has a **cavity in its midsection** which may be empty, may hold rubble, or may hold a gemstone, an ancient relic **or a living creature** — locals believe such things were taken in to protect or imprison them. **Part of the land: strike one down and it will come back.** |
| **Marsh Giant** | Giant-sized woodland spirits, "cousins" to spriggans; not related to Skyrim's giants. Ecologically load-bearing: they knock down dead trees, turn soil, suppress invasive fungi, encourage moss, hinder loggers, boost bird diversity, ward off predators. **The Hist have a long and acrimonious history with them and urge Argonians to avoid them.** Their blood makes paints and inks. |
| **Voriplasm** | A moving pool of green slime that "will strip you to the bone by the second syllable", leaving small bones behind it. A sorcerer once transferred his mind into voriplasm to live for centuries. |
| **Giant Snakes** ("Ample Serpents", *kaj-thux*) | Abundant prey and no competent predators have produced colossal specimens — "a dozen cattle laid end to end". One swallowed an Imperial botanist whole; he cut his way out and died of fleshflies. Their hide protects delicate textiles. |
| **Crocodiles** | Two men long, fast, attacking *inside* town limits at Bogmother. The **Helstrom Crocodile**, an enormous variant, went extinct in the Great Burn; the **Dead-Water Tribe** use their skulls as traps for animals or people. |
| **Sea-drakes** | Crocodilian beasts with paddles for legs, in the deep water off Lilmoth. |
| **Haj Mota** | Large armoured swamp turtle-beast. **Geels-ha butcher eels** are said (Kothringi folklore) to swallow haj mota whole. |
| **Medusas** | In Murkwood. |
| **Lamias** | Infest the caverns beneath Loriasel. |
| **Bog blights** | Argonian dead whose grave-stake has come loose: "dead-and-not-dead things that eat Nagas whole", dangerous in numbers. Warded off with **pahnjees** — racks of oily spoiled meat that mask a living scent. |
| **Wisps and the Wispmother** | Lure travellers to drown; "no mere congregation of swamp vapours" but something intelligent behind an anchor. **Shines** — a harmless cousin — loom around the Imperial ruins of Lilmoth. |
| **Feather-serpents** | "Argonian feather-serpents are said to inhabit the depths of Black Marsh." Unelaborated; a free legendary. |

## Everyday hazards — CANON_EXPLICIT

- **Fleshflies**: red, sand-grain sized, in obscuring clouds; they eat flesh, and
  the border garrison at Slough Point are all missing ears and noses. Repelled by
  a vile-smelling emollient — which only leaves the *larger* insects. They also
  give mounts **sloughskin**.
- **Hackwings**: giant birds with saw-like beaks nearly the size of their bodies.
  "They'll eat you if you don't keep moving. Beggars pounce down and give you a
  nasty chop, then fly off and come back when you're mostly dead from blood loss."
- **Death hoppers** (large frogs; a notably large population around Gideon),
  **kotu gava**, **giant wasps "the size of ponies"**, **giant leeches**,
  **hoarvor**, **fleshfly larvae**, rats that hollow out a swamp leviathan's
  carcass from within.
- **Water**: "a soup of suffering and disease, where a drop of ingested ground
  water can set off a torrent in the bowels." Travellers carry **depth sticks** to
  sound a pond and **dried flower buds** to purify water.
- **Mud**: the black river mud at the border is "a thick gluey paste" that holds a
  standing man's feet fast. Helstrom's **Heart-Mud** is pitch black and the
  region's defining substance.
- **Rain**: "an inferno of foul-smelling, yellow-brown rain", with thunder.
- **Tide**: rivers tide, and a bridge can be under water for four days.
- **Pinnacled mountains** in the interior "that seemed sturdy but suddenly fell
  apart at the slightest ripple in the still water".
- **Burning ground**: after the Great Burn, "choking fumes and vents of burning
  swamp gas began erupting from the earth below. As the gas below the surface
  burned, it would eventually explode." Peat fire is a persistent western hazard.

## Diseases — CANON_EXPLICIT

**Swamp rot** (killed an entire Maran mission in a month), **blood rot**, **the
droops**, **swamp fever**, **rust chancre**, **greenspore**, **sloughskin** (from
fleshflies, affects mounts). Argonians have **natural immunity to poison and
disease**. The **Knahaten Flu** — historical, but fear of it lasted centuries and
a false relapse caused mass panic in the 3E; Argonians call it the
**"Half-Swim Sniffles"** and say they *can* catch it, mildly.

## Domesticated and useful animals — CANON_EXPLICIT

- **Rootworms** — the Underground Express. See
  [../regions/middle-argonia.md](../regions/middle-argonia.md).
- **Lizard-steeds** — the Hist shaped lizards into the forms of other races'
  mounts (horse, pony, wolf, senche, bear, guar, camel) because ordinary mounts
  bog down, catch sloughskin or eat poisonous flora and die. Variants named:
  Wolf-Lizards (hiss instead of howling; some venomous, venom glows in the dark),
  Senche-Lizards, **Chilling Senche-Lizards** from a deep-swamp frost tribe whose
  Hist "drips with icicles", Bear-Lizards, Horse-Lizards (Bright-Throats breed
  their own), Pony-Lizards for children.
- **Guar**: the **Glowgill Guar** of the Archon coast is navy-blue with bright
  blue stripes and reportedly **breathes underwater**; the Gideon/Murkmire golden
  variant is the **Ambergill**.
- **Corimont Mouth-Plovers** — kept by high-status Argonians to clean their fangs.
- **Frogs** — farmed and selected by tone for the **vossa-satl**.
- **Osheeja gars** — used by the Naka-Desh to catch other fish.
- **Fellrunners** — their pale down, worked into a single armband, lets a hunter
  move through Murkwood undetected; Argonian farmers trade sacks of the feathers.
- **Jassa Red slugs** — the acid-secreting slugs used in **slug shaping**.

## Flora — CANON_EXPLICIT

Cypress, mangrove (a ten-mile impenetrable wall near Lilmoth; folklore says they
were once giant spiders punished by the Hist), bamboo, sleeping palms, palm,
bog willow, **flint vine** (cave/xanmeer creeper strong enough to haul weight or
serve as a rope-ladder), **dragon's-tongue** ("beautiful but deadly", especially
common around the **Ultherus Swamp**), **nightbloom**, **somnalius fern**,
**marshweed** (smoked), **torchbugs**, **blue moss** canopies, fields of ferns and
flowered grasses, **lucan mold**, **starblossom** (burned for the Naga-Kur
dream-wallow), **toxicodor** petals, **nirnroot**.

## Minerals — CANON_EXPLICIT

**Stormhold Crystals** (Shadowfen; volatile, contain or channel "evil"; filled
kinds enchanted for healing, buffs, teleportation or instant kill; hollow kinds
store corruption; the great **Storm Crystal**), **vakka stones** (power
pre-Duskfall relics and defences; superficially like welkynd stones), **jadeflint**
and **obverdian**, **bog iron**, **blue jasper** / **bog-blue jasper**, **flint**
("a type of quartz often mined and used by Argonians"), **neesak of scrying**
(divinatory green swamp crystal, common in Murkmire), **red Onkobra clay**,
volcanic glass (pre-Duskfall weapon material; Murkmire locals still skin beasts
with razor shards), **niben-crystal** (common in Blackwood), kwama (the Onkobra
Kwama Mine). **Gold and timber were Soulrest's Imperial-era exports**, halted by
the 3E 427 uprisings.

## Build implications

- Danger in Argonia is overwhelmingly **ambient and attritional** (flies, water,
  disease, mud, tide) punctuated by **set-piece monsters**. Our fixed-danger model
  should express this as a high baseline attrition field with sparse but lethal
  encounters, not as denser enemy spawns.
- **Miregaunts are perfect fixed-danger guardians**: they respawn by canon, they
  contain loot with provenance ("taken in to protect or imprison it"), and they
  are the Hist's own enforcement arm. Use them where a place must never be safe.
- The wamasu's **water electrification** is a first-class hazard for our swimming
  systems: a creature that makes an entire pond lethal argues for approach,
  timing and terrain rather than a straight fight.
- Grave-stakes → bog blights is a **player-caused** danger source, which suits the
  "the world never softens" rule: the player can make a region worse.
- The Great Burn's gas vents give the west a legitimate **environmental hazard
  layer** distinct from creature danger.
