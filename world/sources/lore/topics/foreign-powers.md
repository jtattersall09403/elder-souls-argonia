# Foreign powers in Argonia

Who the outsiders are, what they wanted, and what canon says about where they
stood when it stopped talking. Sources: Lore:House Dres, Lore:Ebonheart Pact,
Lore:East Empire Company, Lore:Cyrodilic Collections, Lore:Accession War,
Lore:Kingdom of Black Marsh, Lore:An-Xileel, Lore:Thalmor, Lore:Archein,

## The Empire — CANON_EXPLICIT

- Held Black Marsh **1E 2837 – 4E ~1**: coasts, borders and the safer interior.
  Never the deep marsh; "it is unknown whether or not these areas even recognise
  Imperial rule" (PGE3, 3E 432).
- Ruled through **Imperial governors in the coastal cities**, each with **Archein**
  advisors; **Archeins governed the rural areas that make up most of the province**.
- The province's Imperial function was **penal**: "the land that had once been the
  home of freedom for Tamriel's criminals became its greatest prison state."
- Imperial economic projects **all failed**. Forty years and millions in gold of
  road, bridge, drainage, anti-smuggling and mercenary work made freight *slower*
  (three weeks → two and a half months, rotten on arrival). "Black Marsh simply
  was, is, and always shall be **unable to sustain a large-scale, cash-crop
  plantation economy**." The only thing that ever worked was Argonian
  subsistence farming plus rafts and rootworms.
- Named Imperial institutions in-province: **Fort Swampmoth** (Legion fortress;
  rumoured emptied during the Oblivion Crisis), **Blackrose Prison**, **White Rose
  Prison**, **Castle Giovesse** (ducal seat, Gideon), the **Governor's Mansion**
  and **courthouse** at Gideon, **Slough Point** census-and-excise on the Cyrodiil
  border, **toll-towns** on the routes, and the **Emperor's fleet guarding Topal
  Bay** against pirates.
- Last Imperial act recorded in-province: **Prime Minister Hierem's secret
  negotiation with the An-Xileel in 4E 47**, apparently proposing an alliance
  against the Thalmor. It ended in the Umbriel catastrophe.
- **Nothing in canon records an Imperial presence in Argonia after 4E 48.**

## House Dres and Morrowind — CANON_EXPLICIT

- Dres built its Great House status on the **slave-raid on Thorn** and ran the
  Argonian slave trade for centuries from **Tear**, its district capital on the
  coastal plain just north of Thornmarsh. **Saltrice** was the crop.
- Enslavement was formally ended by the Pact in 2E 572 and abolished across
  Morrowind by **Helseth in 3E 430** — but **Telvanni kept raiding**, and
  **slavers were still active in Arnesia in the latest Third Era**.
- **Arnesian War, 3E 396**: Morrowind won and annexed Arnesia; Argonians were
  clawing it back by 3E 432.
- **Accession War, 4E 6**: reversed. Argonians sacked Mournhold, reached Scathing
  Bay, nearly finished House Telvanni. **House Redoran** stopped them and became
  Morrowind's dominant House; Hlaalu was expelled from the Grand Council for
  Sadras; the capital moved to Blacklight.
- **4E 201**: Mournhold retaken and largely restored; only **a few Argonian tribes
  in Morrowind's southernmost extremities**, with **occasional frontier patrols**
  (smugglers in the Rift complain of them); the Dunmer regard the invasion as
  having ended "long ago". Argonians still hold the mainland part of **Scathing
  Bay**, denying it to both Morrowind and the Empire.
- → In our era the northern border is **a cold, settled, mutually resented
  frontier**, not an active war. Both sides have grievances two centuries deep and
  neither is currently pressing them.

## The Ebonheart Pact — CANON_EXPLICIT, dead

Founded 2E 572 by Nords, Dunmer and free Argonians after the Second Akaviri
Invasion; governed by a **Great Moot** of equals; only **Shadowfen, Thornmarsh
and Murkmire** joined; **Stormhold was its Black Marsh capital**; it invented the
**Vicecanon**. "It is unknown what became of the Ebonheart Pact, but by the time
of the Tiber War it no longer existed." → It is history in our era, but it left
one durable thing: the **Vicecanon office**, which is the only urban Argonian
governing title canon ever gives us. See the 4E 201 synthesis for why that
matters.

## The Aldmeri Dominion / Thalmor — CANON_EXPLICIT

- 2E 582: the Dominion invaded Shadowfen with the explicit aim of **severing the
  Argonian–Hist bond** and ending Argonian reproduction (Ruuvitar, the Mnemic
  Egg, the Hatching Pools massacre, skin-stealers at Ten-Maur-Wolk). Argonians
  should remember the Dominion as **the enemy that tried to end them as a
  species**, not merely as a rival empire.
- 4E: the **Thalmor are credited with inciting the Argonian uprising against
  southern Morrowind** that preceded secession (Lore:Kingdom of Black Marsh) and
  are one of the standing explanations for the Accession War.
- The Empire in 4E 47 sought an **Argonian alliance against the Thalmor**.
- Canon records **no Thalmor presence inside Black Marsh in the 4E**. The White-
  Gold Concordat (4E 175) binds the Empire, not Argonia.

## The East Empire Company — CANON_EXPLICIT

Imperial chartered monopolist. Archon was "a major stop on the EEC's south-east
lines, with connections to **Lilmoth, Soulrest and Tear**." The Company exported
**saltrice** and controlled import/export of a range of goods; in the 4E it lost
Raven Rock to House Redoran (4E 16) and by 4E 201 is headquartered at **Windhelm**
with a strong Solitude presence and pirate problems in the north.
→ Nothing places the EEC inside Argonia after secession. Its old wharves,
warehouses and bonded stores at Archon, Lilmoth and Soulrest are physical
infrastructure that outlived its charter — excellent for reuse-by-squatters.

## Cyrodilic Collections — CANON_EXPLICIT

An **Imperial** organisation dedicated to **retrieving, restoring and returning
Argonian antiquities to Black Marsh**, funded by benefactors, HQ in Lilmoth
(2E 582), with an alcove at Stormhold; hoped to open a **museum in Black Marsh**.
Its emissaries dealt with local tribes; it hired adventurers.
→ The one foreign institution in canon whose stated purpose Argonians could
approve of — and it was betrayed from within (Kassandra, a Hist-less Dunmer-raised
Argonian who wanted the bond she was denied). A superb template for a foreign
presence that is *tolerated* in our era.

## Build implications

- Argonia's foreign-relations posture is set by **three attempted genocides**:
  the Great Burn (Imperial), the Dominion's Hist-severing, and — worst — **its own
  government's** attempt to purge assimilated Argonians in 4E 48. Suspicion of
  outsiders is earned, and so is suspicion of *nativists*.
- Everything the Empire built that survives is **penal, fiscal or military**:
  prisons, forts, a customs house, toll-towns, a governor's mansion, a courthouse.
  There is no Imperial civic legacy of temples-and-markets to inherit — except at
  Gideon, which is the exception canon explicitly draws.
- The EEC shipping triangle **Archon–Lilmoth–Soulrest–Tear** is the only named
  trade network in the province and should be the skeleton of our maritime
  economy, whoever runs it now.
