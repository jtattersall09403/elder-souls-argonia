# Argonia — dated timeline to 4E 201

Every dated fact canon gives us, in order, with the page it comes from. This is
the spine the extrapolation hangs on: anything we assert about 4E 201 must be a
projection forward from the last dated entry on this list, not a free invention.

## Pre-history

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| Dawn Era | The Ehlnofey war sinks most of the Hist's domain into the Padomaic; "only a small corner left standing, later known as Black Marsh". |
| Merethic | **Stone-Nest period**: Argonians live in xanmeers; **Nisswo priest-kings** rule; Sithis feared purely as Destroyer; live blood sacrifice. Metalworking, volcanic glass, possible Dwemer-inspired work; vakka stones; Hist-bark bows; direct communion with the Hist. Ruins reach as far north as the southern coast of Morrowind's Inner Sea. |
| Merethic | **Duskfall** — the slow decline. Cause never stated. The Hist had forewarning and disagreed on how to meet it: one Hist bargained with **Molag Bal** to wait it out in Coldharbour (Haj Uxith); Mazzatun's Hist ordered its tribe to build in stone forever. The Hist then **stopped speaking to Argonians entirely** until **Jaraleet** rediscovered communion via a Hist-sap decoction. Sithis is reinterpreted as **Raj-Sithis the Changer**; **shunatei** is named and rejected; permanent architecture is abandoned. |
| Merethic (late) | Barsaebics not yet arrived; the xanmeers are **already ancient** when they do. |

## First Era

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| 1E 198 | **Narfinsel Schism** ends at Wendelbek; Aedra-worshipping **Barsaebic Ayleids** are driven east into Black Marsh. They found **Silyanorn** (Stormhold), **Twyllbek** (Gideon), **Archon** and **Thorn**; build **Loriasel** to the Ten Ancestors; enslave Argonians; harvest Argonian souls into **keystones**; are eventually "conquered by the Hist" (legend). Surviving necromancer kings become **Fenlords**. A great battle for the Silyanorn site precedes its founding. |
| 1E 242 | The Ayleid King of Twyllbek bluntly refuses King Glinferen's plea for aid in the Alessian Slave Rebellion — credited with hastening the Ayleid Empire's fall. Black Marsh's Ayleids are spared Alessia's wrath (though the Temple of Sul in Shadowfen was sacked). |
| mid-1E | **Thalthil Dres**'s slave-raid on **Thorn** wins House Dres its Great House status and the leadership of the slave trade. "The Slave-Coffles of Thorn." |
| 1E 1029 | (context) Empress Hestra defeats the Gray Host. |
| **1E 1033** | **Raid of Red Bramman.** Hestra demands the pirate-king's head. The Imperial Navy finds his escape route — a narrow mangrove-screened river emptying into Topal Bay near **Soulrest** — and follows it to his **bandit kingdom near what is now Blackrose**. First reliable outside accounts of Argonian culture. Pirates driven out; the province otherwise left alone. |
| 1E 2200 | **Thrassian Plague**; Argonian freebooters from **Alten Corimont** join the **All Flags Navy** (the ship *Golden Era*) and sail to Thras, never to return. |
| **1E 2811** | **Battle of Argonia.** The last organised Argonian army is destroyed; survivors retreat to **Helstrom**. Reman II's Empire declines to follow into the interior. |
| 1E 2820 | **Xi-Tsei Massacre**: General Bucco's fourth legion annihilated at the foot of a xanmeer. |
| **1E 2828** | **The Great Burn.** Legate **Elissia Mallicius** fires a peat bog outside Stormhold; the fire spreads through an underground network from **Stormhold to Soulrest and Gideon**, burns for over three years, vents burning swamp gas, drives hundreds of species extinct (including the **wraxu** bird and the **Helstrom Crocodile**), destroys whole tribes and ends General Sardecus's campaign. |
| 1E 2833 | Third campaign under **General Lucinia Falco**, headquartered at **Gideon**. She issues **letters of marque to the pirates of the Archon coast** and, with the **Diamond Marines**, takes Lilmoth, Archon, the south-east and much of Middle Argonia. |
| 1E 2834 | The Imperial advance from Gideon halts outside the core. |
| **1E 2836** | The Argonians **abruptly stop fighting** — no armistice, no explanation. They bury their weapons and return to daily life. Imperial scholars blamed Hist worship; Lore:Blackwater War blames Argonian tribal infighting. |
| **1E 2837** | The **Imperial Province of Black Marsh** is founded over the northern and eastern marches. The Empire, unsure what to do with the place, builds **prisons** (Blackrose, White Rose). |
| 1E 2899 | Empress **Tavia** imprisoned at **Castle Giovesse**, Gideon, for treason. |
| 1E 2920 | Tavia's death; Reman III assassinated (his Kothringi knight **Zuuk** later founds a Shadowfen town). Four-Score War: **Warchief Ulaqth of Lilmoth** commands the right flank at Bodrum. |

## Second Era

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| early 2E | Potentate **Versidue-Shaie**'s battlemage **Pelladil Direnni** raises **Blackrose Prison** in a single day, with an army of stone atronachs, from the rubble of the Lilmothiit ruins. |
| ~1E 700+ | Boethiah-worshipping Dunmer dissidents build **Tenmorvuk / Ten-Maur-Wolk**; later a Dunmer slave camp. |
| 2E 430s | Second Empire collapses; **Blackrose Prison is abandoned** and reclaimed by the swamp; the **Blackguards** — descendants of its inmates — take it over and become Murkmire's largest criminal organisation. |
| **2E 560–603** | **Knahaten Flu.** Begins at **Stormhold**; kills all infected non-Argonians; 43 years. Exterminates the **Kothringi** and the **Lilmothiit**; the **Cantemiric Velothi** too. Drives every outsider from the province except **House Dres**, whose economy depends on slaves. Origin still disputed (vengeful Argonian shaman via the Hist / natural / an Ayleid weapon against the Kothringi). |
| 2E 561 | Stillrise Village's Kothringi bargain with **Clavicus Vile** and become immortal skeletons. |
| 2E 563 | The **Crimson Ship** leaves Black Marsh with Kothringi refugees; turned back at every port. |
| 2E 572 | **Second Akaviri Invasion.** **Heita-Meen** kills Councillor Glathis Dres at Thorn, takes the Archein guard by duel, marches to **Stormhold** and wins the **Shellbacks**; **Keshu the Black Fin** and the **Black Fin Legion** answer Jorunn's call. The Akaviri are driven into the sea. The **Ebonheart Pact** is founded; Dunmer formally end Argonian enslavement (**House Telvanni refuses and secedes**). Only **Shadowfen, Thornmarsh and Murkmire** join; Stormhold is named Pact capital of the affiliated regions. The **Vicecanon** office is invented at Stormhold. |
| 2E 582 | Three Banners War year. Aldmeri Dominion invades Shadowfen (Ruuvitar's plan to sever the Hist bond via the **Mnemic Egg**); Alten Corimont run by pirate **Captain One-Eye**; **Keshu** governs Gideon; **Cyrodilic Collections** works out of Lilmoth; Murkmire remains autonomous; Blackrose rumoured in ruins. |
| 2E 864 | (context) All Kothringi believed extinct. |

## Third Era

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| ~3E 1– | Tiber Septim takes the borders easily and deliberately avoids the inner swamps. The province is again a **prison state** whose coasts the Empire holds. |
| 3E 80 | Warlady **Ioa** of Lilmoth. |
| 3E 109 | Queen **Hellena** of Lilmoth executes the Argonian Priest-King and marries Prince **Magnus Septim**, who becomes King of Lilmoth and later Emperor Magnus I (3E 140). Lilmoth fights for the Empire in the **War of the Red Diamond** (3E 120–137); Argonian forces campaign as far as Skyrim. |
| ~3E 145 | **Rockpark**'s collapse (pestilence, plague, curse). |
| 3E 173 | **Lilmoth's Hist goes rogue and is purged by the other Hist.** A new tree grows from a surviving root fragment; its roots spread through lower Lilmoth. |
| **3E 396** | **Arnesian War.** An Argonian slave revolt in southern Dres lands kills the Dunmer trader **Roris** (canonised). Morrowind wins and annexes **Arnesia**. |
| 3E 389–399 | Imperial Simulacrum: every major city has a monarch — Germanus (Helstrom, called *the capital*), Demia (Gideon), Claudios (Blackrose), Herula (Archon), Artomeda (Lilmoth), Heciana (Soulrest), Aphiana (Stormhold), Tibus (Thorn). City **rivalries** are recorded (Helstrom–Stormhold, Gideon–Soulrest, Archon–Lilmoth, Archon–Thorn, Blackrose–Lilmoth). Followers of **Seth** hold vast control in Gideon. |
| ~3E 398 | (The Argonian Account) Forty years of Imperial road-building have made freight *slower*: two and a half months, rotten on arrival. Argonian rafts and rootworms do it in two weeks, unspoiled. Archein plantations work serfs to death and sell children. **Naga highwaymen** rob the roads. When the roads collapse, the Naga return to the inner swamp and the Archeins go bankrupt. |
| 3E 427 | Tribal **uprisings destroy plantations and mines at Soulrest** and halt exports of **gold and timber**; the Imperial **Expeditionary Force** is sent to quell them. Soulrest is then accepted as capital of Black Marsh. Stormhold's prison is run by the mad Warden **Quintus Varus**, who has "Tunnel Rats" dig for Stormhold Crystals. |
| 3E 430 | King Helseth abolishes slavery in Morrowind. Argonian–Dunmer relations improve; border clashes continue as Argonians reclaim Arnesian War ground. |
| ~late 3E | **Blackrose rebels against the Third Empire** and raises a large army; Empress Morihatha's battlemage **Welloc** breaks it with *vampiric cloud*. |
| **3E 432** | PGE3's snapshot: banditry declining, criminals mostly native Naga, drug-smuggling rumours, the Emperor's fleet guarding Topal Bay against pirates "never truly eliminated", Imperial governors on the coasts advised by **Archeins** who also govern the rural majority, no supervision of the inner swamp at all, and a **recent Blackrose Prison break** that put the worst murderers, thieves and **political revolutionaries** into the marsh. |
| **3E 433** | **Oblivion Crisis.** The Hist foresee it, call Argonians home and **physically alter them** — faster, stronger, tougher. Argonians charge *into* the Oblivion Gates so ferociously that Dagon's lieutenants close them. Black Marsh emerges stronger and more unified than any other province. The **An-Xileel** form during the Crisis and take the credit; **Fort Swampmoth**'s legions are rumoured recalled. |

## Fourth Era — the era we build in

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| **4E 1–5** | Black Marsh **secedes**, renaming itself Argonia. Lore:Kingdom of Black Marsh states the secession followed **Thalmor incitement of an uprising against southern Morrowind**. The An-Xileel sack the Imperial colonies. |
| 4E 5 | The **Red Year**: Baar Dau destroys Vivec City; Red Mountain erupts. |
| **4E 6** | **Accession War** at its height. Argonian armies sack **Mournhold**, reach the **Scathing Bay** (where they perform "rituals of unknown purpose") and nearly destroy **House Telvanni**. **House Redoran**'s re-raised army halts them. Argonians occupy southern Morrowind; some tribes settle it. |
| 4E 6–28 | The advance stops. "Since then the Argonians have shown no interest in conquering any more land." They do, however, **prevent the Empire from reclaiming that territory**. |
| 4E 8 | The **rogue Hist of Lilmoth** has cut itself off again; it speaks only to the An-Xileel and the **Wild Ones**. The An-Xileel in Lilmoth **take counsel from no other Hist**. |
| 4E 15 | Potentate Ocato assassinated; **Stormcrown Interregnum** begins. |
| 4E 16 | Solstheim ceded to Morrowind for Dunmer refugees. |
| 4E 22 | Titus Mede I takes the Imperial City. Thalmor secede; Aldmeri Dominion reformed. |
| **4E 47** | Imperial **Prime Minister Hierem** makes a **secret trip to Black Marsh** to negotiate with the An-Xileel — apparently proposing an **alliance against the Thalmor**. He seals the agreement with a ritual at the Lilmoth city tree involving the word **"Umbriel"**. |
| **4E 48** | **The Umbriel catastrophe.** The An-Xileel, through the rogue Hist, summon Umbriel to "exterminate all foreign taint and *assimilated* Argonians". They and the Wild Ones plan to hide and return once Black Marsh is "clean". Umbriel slips their control: the **Slaughter at Lilmoth** destroys the city; Umbriel crosses Murkmire killing everyone beneath it and raising them as undead; **Gideon and Stormhold are overrun by the undead army despite not being under its shadow** ("light" damage relative to those on the path); the island then goes north-west through Morrowind toward the Imperial City, where it is destroyed. **Archwarden Qajalil** is the An-Xileel's named officer of the year; the party is led by **The Organism**, a ruling council based in Lilmoth. |
| after 4E 48 | Lore:Accession War: "This disaster appears to have **broken the hold the An-Xileel held over the Argonian people**, and the mysterious party **receives no mention in the following centuries**." Lore:An-Xileel: "The status of the An-Xileel following the Umbriel Crisis is **unknown**." |
| 4E 150 | A small force of **Argonian raiders lands on Solstheim** and is repelled by the Redoran Guard of Raven Rock. |
| before 4E 151 | The Dunmer retake **Mournhold**. |
| 4E 171–175 | The **Great War** (Empire v. Aldmeri Dominion) — Argonia is not recorded as a participant. |
| **4E 187** | The **Shadowscale training facility at Archon is shut down**. Listener **Alisanne Dupre** of Cheydinhal and **Rasha** plan to revive it in response to the Dark Brotherhood's decline. |
| **4E 201** | Mournhold's restoration ongoing but secure enough that the Redoran garrison has been reassigned. Only **a few Argonian tribes** remain in Morrowind's southernmost extremities, with **occasional patrols along the frontier**; smugglers in the Rift complain about them; the Dunmer consider the invasion to have ended "long ago". **The last known living Shadowscale dies**, and the order may now be extinct; a Shadowscale in Skyrim states the order "was no longer a fully functioning group". Gideon is recorded as an **active settlement** "later in the Fourth Era". |

## What the timeline forces on us

1. **The single most important fact for our era is 4E 48**, not 4E 6. The
   province's own government tried to purge its own people, the weapon turned on
   them, and the three largest cities in the north and south were killed or
   gutted. 4E 201 is **153 years after that**, which is roughly the distance from
   the Napoleonic wars to today: within recorded memory, outside living memory,
   and long enough to rebuild.
2. **Every named 4E 201 data point about Argonia is about withdrawal**: patrols
   thinning on the Morrowind border, Shadowscales dying out, the Archon facility
   closed, the An-Xileel unmentioned for centuries. Canon's own trajectory for
   4E-201 Argonia is *quiet and inward-turned*, not expansionist.
3. **The province has been "cleared out and repopulated" three times** — the Great
   Burn, the Knahaten Flu, Umbriel. That is a legitimate structural reason for
   ruins-in-layers, sparse population, and old infrastructure with no owner.
4. **Nothing in canon says who governs Argonia in 4E 201.** That is the central
   gap and it is deliberately open. See
   [../extrapolation/gap-register.md](../extrapolation/gap-register.md) §1.
