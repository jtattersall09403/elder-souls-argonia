# Umbriel — the 4E 48 catastrophe

Covers quest-plan lore source L15. Sources: Lore:Umbriel, Lore:An-Xileel,
Lore:Black Marsh, Lore:Soul-spinner, Books:Greg Keyes Novels/Umbriel
(fetched 2026-08-28). The primary texts are the Greg Keyes novels *The
Infernal City* and *Lord of Souls*, as summarised on UESP.

## What Umbriel is — CANON_EXPLICIT

- A **floating island-city in a pocket realm of Oblivion**, split from
  Clavicus Vile's Fields of Regret by **Umbra**. Cone-shaped, narrow end down:
  the watery **Sump** at the bottom (souls processed, people born, food
  grown), the tree-covered **Fringe Gyre** round the rim, lords' palaces at
  the top; buildings jut from rock, castles hang from cables, glass-like
  towers on the rim (Lore:Umbriel).
- Powered by a new **Ingenium** built by the Dunmer **Vuhon**: **people
  killed beneath the city fuel it**, sustaining the city and satisfying a
  pact with Umbra (Lore:Umbriel).
- Its people are born adult from recycled souls, live ten to fifteen years,
  and speak Umbrielic (near-Ehlnofex). The Fringe Gyre's trees are **akin to
  the Hist** — which is what made the summoning possible (Lore:Umbriel;
  Books:Greg Keyes Novels/Umbriel).

## The summoning, 4E 48 — CANON_EXPLICIT

- The **An-Xileel summoned it**, using the **rogue Hist tree of Lilmoth** to
  speak to its tree-"cousins" aboard, **plotting to exterminate all foreign
  taint and "assimilated" Argonians in Black Marsh**; they and the Wild Ones
  planned to hide, wait out the carnage, and return once the province was
  "clean" (Lore:An-Xileel).
- The Imperial minister **Hierem** was implicated: he performed a ritual at
  Lilmoth's city tree involving the word "Umbriel" (Lore:An-Xileel, citing
  *Lord of Souls*).
- Umbriel **first appeared far off the coast of Black Marsh, south of
  Lilmoth**, then moved over the city; its forces **killed Lilmoth's
  residents** (Lore:Umbriel; Books:Greg Keyes Novels/Umbriel).
- "The city did this for them, but soon began to pursue its own agenda after
  the An-Xileel lost control over it" (Lore:An-Xileel) — its true goal was to
  free itself from Vile's realm using the **White-Gold Tower** (Lore:Umbriel).

## The path — CANON_EXPLICIT, and deliberately vague inside Black Marsh

What canon actually states about the route, in full:

- Appeared **south of Lilmoth**, off the coast; moved over **Lilmoth**
  (Lore:Umbriel).
- Then "started on a **northwestern path** towards White-Gold Tower in the
  Imperial City, **moving through Black Marsh and Morrowind** in the process"
  (Lore:An-Xileel).
- Waypoints named by canon: **Lilmoth (southern Black Marsh) → Scathing Bay
  (north-central Morrowind, the drowned crater of Vivec) → the Imperial City**
  (Lore:Umbriel; Lore:Soul-spinner).

**No canon source names any Black Marsh waypoint between Lilmoth and
Morrowind.** No city, prison or region on the interior leg is identified —
not Blackrose, not the Rose, not Stormhold. Any specific overflight claim
inside the province is `EXTRAPOLATED` at best (see
[prisons.md](prisons.md) for the Rose's tag). A Lilmoth→Morrowind
north/northwestern corridor is *consistent with* passing over eastern or
central Black Marsh; that is as far as canon goes.

## The undead it raised — CANON_EXPLICIT

- **Soul-spinners**, possibly-Daedric creatures of Umbriel, caught the souls
  of those who died beneath the city; their moth-like larvae flew down and
  animated the soulless corpses as undead — "**wormies**" (the Imperial
  soldiers' name) or **taskers** (Umbriel's name) (Lore:Soul-spinner).
- "Those slain by the floating city were resurrected as an **invincible horde
  of undead** … they **began to rise around Umbriel and follow its
  trajectory** from … Lilmoth … to the Scathing Bay … and the Imperial City"
  (Lore:An-Xileel; Lore:Soul-spinner). Many were Argonians, "but the army
  consisted of anything that came in Umbriel's path"; those who died fighting
  them rose instantly.
- They were **not mindless**: intelligent, malicious, capable of speech, at
  home **underwater** (Scathing Bay, Lake Rumare), and tactically able
  (ambushes near Cheydinhal, staying out of siege-weapon range)
  (Lore:Soul-spinner).
- **Canon does not state what became of the horde after Umbriel's removal.**
  Any surviving remnant population (e.g. undead left in structures along the
  Black Marsh leg) is `EXTRAPOLATED`.

## The departure — CANON_EXPLICIT

Through Annaïg Hoïnart, Mere-Glim, Prince Attrebus Mede and the Dunmer mage
Sul, Umbriel's threat was ended (Lore:An-Xileel): the city was **transported
to the realm of the Hist, where it settled deep into the boggy ground**,
supported by its covering of trees (Lore:Umbriel, citing *Lord of Souls*).
It never physically crashed in Tamriel. The 4E 201 **Sleeping Tree** in
Whiterun Hold is rumoured (one legend among several) to have grown from a
spore fallen from the floating island (Lore:Umbriel).

## Build implications

- The provincial trauma is **Lilmoth plus a corridor**: total slaughter at
  Lilmoth, then a moving swathe north/northwest across the province with an
  undead army rising in its wake. Memorials, family stories and depopulated
  ground belong along that corridor — placed by us, since canon names no
  interior waypoint (`EXTRAPOLATED` placements, era rule per the owner's
  WWI-distance directive).
- The An-Xileel's complicity — they *summoned* it and hid — is the province's
  buried political scandal, canon-explicit and 150 years cold. See
  [../an-xileel.md](../an-xileel.md).
- Umbriel undead are canonically **water-capable and intelligent** — a
  legitimate template for any 4E 48-derived undead we place, and notably
  suited to drowned interiors.
- The Deepmire **Umbriel memorial** (settlement register) is the province's
  formal remembrance site; the Umbriel Witness Society (UW line) hangs off
  this dossier.
