# Soulrest

**Canon (UESP Lore:Soulrest, fetched 2026-08-22):**

- Major city "on the southwest coast next to the **Topal Bay** … on a
  peninsula that extends into the west wrapping around Topal Bay", where the
  bay meets the Southern Sea. Islands lie south of the city.
- "A quiet port-town that leaves the crashing sound of the sea behind its
  gates; its coastal access brings a vast influx of foreign goods and
  aesthetics." **Capital of Black Marsh during the Third Era.**
- "Just south of the city is the start of a river that drains out into the sea
  and travels northeast into the interior."
- **Red Bramman** (notorious 1E pirate-lord) operated here — escaping the
  Alessian navy "via a narrow, winding river near Soulrest, encased by a dense
  thicket of mangroves". Ancient wraxu birds etched in local xanmeers.
- Notable locales: Soulrest Palace, Brotherhood of Seth, Mages Guild.
