# Soulrest

**Canon (UESP Lore:Soulrest, fetched 2026-08-22):**

- Major city "on the southwest coast next to the **Topal Bay** … on a
  peninsula that extends into the west wrapping around Topal Bay", where the
  bay meets the Southern Sea. Islands lie south of the city.
- "A quiet port-town that leaves the crashing sound of the sea behind its
  gates; its coastal access brings a vast influx of foreign goods and
  aesthetics." **Capital of Black Marsh during the Third Era.**
- "Just south of the city is the start of a river that drains out into the sea
  and travels northeast into the interior."
- **Red Bramman** (notorious 1E pirate-lord) operated here — escaping the
  Alessian navy "via a narrow, winding river near Soulrest, encased by a dense
  thicket of mangroves". Ancient wraxu birds etched in local xanmeers.
- Notable locales: Soulrest Palace, Brotherhood of Seth, Mages Guild.

**Build implications:** our WSW-coast placement matches; give it a Bramman
hidden-river smuggler network (plan §24 pirate systems); Third-Era capital
status justifies Imperial administrative layers; most demographically mixed
zone (19% Khajiit prior) → mixed dockside districts.

**Confidence:** CANON_FIXED (position, capital status, Bramman).

## Sweep addendum (UESP re-fetch 2026-08-24)

**CANON_EXPLICIT additions**

- Neighbour: **Chasepoint** (E).
- Argonians have lived in the Soulrest region "since at least ancient times, back
  when the **wraxu** birds were still alive" — the wraxu went extinct in the
  **Great Burn (1E 2828)** and their feathers had a cultural connection to Sithis
  (they envelop the **Gaze of Sithis** helm). Local xanmeers are etched with them.
- **The Great Burn reached here.** The Stormhold peat fire spread through "a vast
  underground network that stretches across the western portion of Black Marsh,
  from Stormhold to Soulrest and Gideon to the south". Soulrest sits on the far
  end of that burn network — **burn-scar geology, gas vents and a dead-forest
  history are canon here**, not invention.
- **Soulrest had an extractive economy, and it collapsed.** "Sometime in 3E 427,
  the tribes of Black Marsh started a series of uprisings that **destroyed
  plantations and mines in Soulrest**, all the while halting exports of **gold and
  timber**. The **Expeditionary Force** was sent in by the Empire to quell these
  tribes. At this time, Soulrest was accepted to be the capital of Black Marsh."
  → The only named **mining and timber** industry in the province, its only named
  export commodities, and a documented tribal insurgency against them six years
  before the Empire lost the province entirely.
- "At some point in time, there was a **struggle for regional hegemony** in
  Soulrest" — an unexplained internal power contest, free for us to define.
- Imperial Simulacrum: **Queen Heciana**; **rivalry with Gideon**.
- The **Southern Sea**'s pirates "keep a much lower profile" than the Abecean's to
  avoid Altmer and Maormer warships, and **a large portion of these seafarers are
  Khajiit** — which independently supports the high Khajiit share in Soulrest's
  demographic prior.
- Islands lie south of the city; the **Quin'rawl Peninsula** across Topal Bay is
  itself a smuggling coast.

**Notes for authoring**

- Soulrest is the province's **ex-capital and ex-industrial port**: an
  administrative apparatus with nothing left to administer, and mine-and-mill
  country behind it that the tribes burned down. That is a far more interesting
  premise than "cosmopolitan port", and it explains why the capital moved and why
  the hinterland is hostile.
- The Bramman river is a canon **navigable smuggler route from Topal Bay to the
  Blackrose lakes**, screened by mangroves and known to have been sailed by a
  fleet. It should be a real, findable, contested waterway — the single best
  piece of pirate/smuggler geography in the province.
- Gold and timber give Soulrest a loot and trade-goods identity distinct from every
  other city, and abandoned mine workings give it a dungeon family.
