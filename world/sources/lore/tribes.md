# Argonian tribes and peoples

**Canon (UESP via corpus extract; page names cited):**

## Naga and the Naga-Kur (Lore:Naga, Lore:Naga-Kur)

- Nagas: "a little-known breed of Argonian that live in the deep parts of
  Black Marsh" — 7–8 ft tall, "huge mouths filled with dripping needle-like
  fangs", snake-featured ('puff adders').
- During Imperial plantation attempts they "generally acted as highway
  robbers; following the cessation of these efforts, they have since left the
  roads and gone back into the inner swamps". → Encounter design: Naga road
  ambush history on old plantation fringes; present-day inner-swamp territory.
- **Naga-Kur ("Dead-Water Tribe")**: proud traditionalist warriors of northern
  Murkmire, village Dead-Water Village, "control much of the inner swamps…
  greatly feared by surrounding villages"; hostile to outsiders; adorn arms
  with bones of fallen comrades. → Drives the `naga-kur-deeps` culture zone.

## Murkmire tribes (Lore:Tribes of Murkmire book series, fetched 2026-08-23)

Hurrent's survey counted "at least twelve distinct tribal groups in Murkmire
alone … scores more exist" — tribal density far beyond the named set. Named:

- **Bright-Throats** — diplomats and artisans; village with hatching pools,
  Tree-Minder; the "friendly port of contact" tribe.
- **Ghost People (Veeskhleel)** — pale, nocturnal, dwell "entirely in the
  dark", stalk and abduct the unwary; territory Veeskhleel-Tzel / Blight Bog;
  horror-flavour tribe.
- **Black-Tongues (Kota-Vimleel)** — soft-spoken master alchemists who mark
  territory with alchemical flasks, kill trespassers without hesitation, and
  "dedicate almost all their resources" to producing **Shadowscales**.
- **Root-House People (Tum-Taleel)** — belligerent village-takers: attack
  peaceful villages, drive off inhabitants, occupy their huts; "own" the
  **Keel-Sakka river bridge** and toll/ambush it.
- **Miredancers (Gee-Rusleel)** — pious, pleasant, reclusive; amber/eggshell
  artisans, "peerless navigators, master weavers, skilled cartographers";
  their piety earns the right to name a **Sap-Speaker** (Hist intermediary).
- **Dead-Water (Naga-Kur)** — see above.
- **Root-Whisper** — destroyed by a Barsaebic Ayleid incursion; souls kept in
  the Remnant of Argon at the cost of their Hist's sleep; village between the
  pre-Duskfall twin temples Xul-Thuxis and Vakka-Bok (ON:Root-Whisper Village).

Region facts (Lore:Murkmire): the **Keel-Sakka River** runs here; Murkmire had
no central authority and stayed autonomous of the Ebonheart Pact (2E); Umbriel
passed over in 4E 48, killing inhabitants below (population history for our
4E 201 state). Travel colour: cypress and torchbugs, flesh-fly clouds
thickening toward the interior, "the deep murk devours outsiders".

Era: ESO (2E) — tribal survival to 4E 201 is plausible continuity, mark
AGENT_AUTHORED per tribe when placed.

## Archeins (Lore:Gideon, Lore:Thorn)

Kleptocratic Argonian tribe operating within city politics — advisors/rulers
in Gideon, village-owners outside Thorn. A "collaborator/landlord" Argonian
class for fringe-city politics, distinct from free tribes.

## Shadowscales (Lore:Shadowfen, Lore:Archon)

Shadow-born Argonians raised as Dark Brotherhood assassins; training facility
attested at Archon; tied to Shadowfen tradition.

**Build implications:** heartland subdivides into Hist-bound tribes (centre)
vs Naga-Kur deeps (south); tribal variety feeds Phase 11 settlement grammars;
Archein politics gives fringe cities internal antagonists without Imperials.

## Sweep addendum (UESP re-fetch 2026-08-24)

### Why tribes differ: *gloor* — CANON_EXPLICIT

An Argonian's appearance and physiology are **almost solely determined by their
Hist**, through the sap ingested on their Naming Day. Argonians are
"adaptable-by-induction to their Hist's **gloor**". Ancestry is irrelevant:
a hatchling raised under a tribe's Hist shows *that* tribe's traits. Traits
**fade with distance** from the tribe's Hist and **return** on coming home.
→ Tribe = Hist = morphology = craft = danger profile. Full treatment in
[topics/hist-and-sap.md](topics/hist-and-sap.md). This is the mechanism that
makes our regional Argonian variety legitimate, and it means **every distinct
tribe we place requires a Hist to be placed with it.**

### Physiologically distinct breeds — CANON_EXPLICIT (Lore:Argonian)

Named as morphologically distinct: **Agaceph, Hapsleet, Mihuitleel, Naga,
Naka-Desh, Paatru, Sarpa**. Note that "Saxhleel" and "Naga" are sometimes held to
be different peoples: Naga are taller, wider-faced, 7–8 ft, beady-eyed, needle-
fanged.

| Breed | Where | Canon |
|---|---|---|
| **Agaceph** | Middle Argonia, exclusive | Needle-faced; "a whole rainbow of scales" — ruby, lemon, bright green, gem-like purple, blue, orange. Travel by razor-thin one-person rafts propelled by the tail. Educated (they looted the dead Maran monastery at Umpholo for its books) and, like "most simple day labourers in Black Marsh", **accomplished Illusionists** — a stranded Imperial was paralysed by two casual fishermen. Sardonic and incurious about the outside world. Will help a stranded outsider. |
| **Paatru** | Middle Argonia, exclusive | Toad-like. Would have killed the same outsider on sight. |
| **Sarpa** | Middle Argonia, exclusive | Winged. Likewise. |
| **Naka-Desh** (Riverbacks) | Blackwood | Wide faces, large eyes, **broad webs on forearms and throat**. Perceived as secretive, but really just see no benefit in travelling beyond their Hist's roots. Not curious; don't care for cultural exchange — **except riddles**. Use **osheeja gars** to catch fish. Were close friends of the Kothringi and admired their music. |
| **Naga (tribeless)** | Inner swamps | "The **majority of the native brigands and highwaymen** are tribeless Naga." Their cruelty "does not make them evil, nor are they universally reviled" — Nagas are not a monolith. |

### Tribal politics — CANON_EXPLICIT

- **Grudges expire.** "Slights committed between Argonian tribes are forgiven
  relatively easily", partly because of **reincarnation across tribes**: a
  Bright-Throat may return as a Black-Tongue. "To hate each other is to hate
  themselves, because they are all people of the root." The Bright-Throats still
  bond with the Root-House People **who once burned their village down**.
- **But collaboration does not expire.** The **Archeins** are "widely hated by
  their fellow Argonians" as kleptocrats who sold their own into slavery and
  exploited their land for the Empire — "**pompous, assimilated slaver
  kleptocrats**". Their fate after the Oblivion Crisis and the secession is
  **explicitly unknown in canon** (Lore:Archein) — one of our sharper gaps.
- Alliances are made through **bonding ceremonies**, not treaties: the
  Bright-Throats invite suitors from the Black-Tongues, Moss-Skins and Root-House
  People, pair them, feast them on phlegmwine, and **cancelling a ceremony is a
  diplomatic rupture**.
- Village leadership offices (tree-minder, sap-speaker, root-herald, kaal,
  raj-kaal, naheesh, deelith, grave-singer, egg-tender) are catalogued in
  [topics/material-culture.md](topics/material-culture.md).

**Build note:** the forgiveness rule is important for tone. Inter-tribal conflict
in Argonia should be **frequent, violent and non-permanent** — raids, seizures and
sieges that end without lasting hatred — while conflict with *collaborators* and
outsiders is **cold and long**. That asymmetry is unusual and worth playing.
