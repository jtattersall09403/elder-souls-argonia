# Argonian tribes and peoples

**Canon (UESP via corpus extract; page names cited):**

## Naga and the Naga-Kur (Lore:Naga, Lore:Naga-Kur)

- Nagas: "a little-known breed of Argonian that live in the deep parts of
  Black Marsh" — 7–8 ft tall, "huge mouths filled with dripping needle-like
  fangs", snake-featured ('puff adders').
- During Imperial plantation attempts they "generally acted as highway
  robbers; following the cessation of these efforts, they have since left the
  roads and gone back into the inner swamps". → Encounter design: Naga road
  ambush history on old plantation fringes; present-day inner-swamp territory.
- **Naga-Kur ("Dead-Water Tribe")**: proud traditionalist warriors of northern
  Murkmire, village Dead-Water Village, "control much of the inner swamps…
  greatly feared by surrounding villages"; hostile to outsiders; adorn arms
  with bones of fallen comrades. → Drives the `naga-kur-deeps` culture zone.

## Murkmire tribes (Lore:Tribes of Murkmire + ON book series)

Attested tribes: **Bright-Throats** (Bright-Throat Village), **Ghost People
(Veeskhleel)**, **Black-Tongues**, **Root-House People (Tum-Taleel)**,
**Miredancers**, **Dead-Water**; plus Root-Whisper Village. Era: ESO (2E) —
tribal survival to 4E 201 is plausible continuity, mark AGENT_AUTHORED per
tribe when placed.

## Archeins (Lore:Gideon, Lore:Thorn)

Kleptocratic Argonian tribe operating within city politics — advisors/rulers
in Gideon, village-owners outside Thorn. A "collaborator/landlord" Argonian
class for fringe-city politics, distinct from free tribes.

## Shadowscales (Lore:Shadowfen, Lore:Archon)

Shadow-born Argonians raised as Dark Brotherhood assassins; training facility
attested at Archon; tied to Shadowfen tradition.

**Build implications:** heartland subdivides into Hist-bound tribes (centre)
vs Naga-Kur deeps (south); tribal variety feeds Phase 11 settlement grammars;
Archein politics gives fringe cities internal antagonists without Imperials.
